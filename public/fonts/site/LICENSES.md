# Site fonts

Self-hosted latin and latin-ext WOFF2 subsets, served as downloaded from the Google Fonts CDN
(fetched 2026-10-01), declared in `src/styles/fonts.css`. All SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org), used unmodified. The remaining site faces (Fira Sans 300-600 and
italic 400, Fira Sans Condensed, Source Serif 4) are shared with the Gipfelbuch and documented in
`public/fonts/gipfelbuch/LICENSES.md`.
