Method
  State: yaw, pitch, roll, fScale, eye E/N/U, k1 (optional), plus ONE nuisance: an edge-cue offset along the
  cue normal (px, prior 0 ± 3). WP-C measured a −1.5…−2.7 px contour offset at GT in every band. The
  nuisance absorbs it so it is not read as pose; fitted values range from −3.9 to +0.2 px.
  Residuals (whitened; Cauchy on data rows, quadratic priors):
    skyline  f·(el_obs − horizon(az; eye)), σ 2 px, scale 4 px, all samples weigh like 60 (pose6dof/eye.ts
             convention). horizon(az; eye) is linearised in the eye by central differences of horizons at eye
             ± 5 m (7 horizons per HorizonsAtEyes call). An outer loop re-linearises at the new eye with a
             proximal trust term (σ 10 m) until the eye moves < 1 m: Gauss–Newton form of the variable
             projection in refineEyeFromSkyline.
    cues     point [dx, dy]; edge (along n) − offset; level f·(el_obs − el(world from eye)); shore = signed shore
             distance of the lake-plane hit ÷ its image gradient (px). Each group (kind:source) is capped at an
             effective count: edge 40, level 20, shore 20, point 60 (WP-G), pins uncapped. Non-pin point cues
             get the DEM σ (3 + 0.001·d m) added in quadrature.
    priors   GPS: σH horizontal and σV vertical around eyePrior.eye0. Standing height: eye − DEM(eye.xy) −
             1.6, σ 2 m above and 0.5 m below. These two are WP-B's altitude-contour term split into its
             measurements. Also: focal (WP-B focalPrior), k1 0 ± 0.05, rotation 5/3/3° around cam0.
  Freedom rules: eye freed only with ≥ 6 cues nearer than 2 km; k1 only with ≥ 6 corner cues; the
  principal point is never freed.
  Gate (gate.ts):
    - skyline RMS (|r| < 30 px) ≤ before + 0.5 px, inlier fraction drop ≤ 0.05;
    - cue RMS no worse (+0.05);
    - |eye shift| ≤ 3σH; focal ≤ 4σ from the prior;
    - holdout evidence required. Either held-out pins (callback) must improve their median, with p90 no
      worse than +1 px, or, without pins, a 2-fold spatial cue cross-check (4×4 checkerboard; fit one colour,
      score the other at cam0 vs the fit, both ways) must improve the sum with no fold worse by > 0.25 px;
    - no evidence ⇒ reject.
  concordRefine: refuses at LOW confidence (field/fit.ts isLowConfidence, the rule WP-E uses). Otherwise:
  extract cues → solve → re-extract at the new camera (WP-C searches only ±12 px) → solve → cross-check
  → gate. It returns ctx.cam unchanged unless accepted.

API vs plan (additive / clarifying only)
  - JointInput adds: start (initial camera; cam0 stays the prior and "before" reference); ground ((e, n) →
    z in cam0's frame, the standing-height term; default eyePrior.isoBand.ground); frame (lake level → z for
    shore cues without world).
  - cues are JointCue = Cue & {residualPx?, conf?, world?}. For "edge" cues (u, v) is the PREDICTED
    contour point and the observed edge is (u, v) − residualPx·n (WP-C MatchedCue convention). Point, level
    and shore cues carry the observed pixel. Level/shore use `world` (WP-C WaterCue) when present.
  - focal: {fPx, sigmaPx, basisLongPx?}. px at the 1600 long-side basis unless basisLongPx is given
    (WP-B focalPrior returns px of the full image: pass basisLongPx = max(w, h)).
  - eyePrior must be in cam0.eye's frame. WP-B's is fix-centred with absolute z: in the engine frame (eye
    [0, 0, eyeAlt], absolute) no conversion is needed; in the eval frame subtract the GT eye altitude
    (solve-eval.ts does).
  - JointResult adds: cam0, freed, eyeShift, eyeShiftSigmaH, focalZ, edgeBiasPx, skylineInlierBefore/After,
    counts, iterations, outer, horizonCalls, converged, ms. cov is 9×9 in PARAM_NAMES order (0 for fixed).
    sigma is keyed by parameter name.
  - JointOpts adds the tunables listed in JOINT_DEFAULTS (all fixed a priori, not tuned on pins).
  - gate(r, holdout?, extra?): holdout may return a number (median) or {medPx, p90Px}. extra carries
    {crossCheck, opts}.
  - "Near cues are the only ones that may move the eye" is implemented as a precondition: the eye is freed
    only with ≥ 6 near cues. Far cues and the skyline stay exactly modelled at the moving eye. Zeroing
    their eye derivatives would make the LM cost inconsistent: a 10 m move shifts a 2 km cue by ~6 px.
  - Plan said the solver "may call lmSolve from pose6dof/solve.ts". It uses its own dense LM instead:
    skyline, edge and shore rows are not correspondences.

Known issues
  - Pins: all 34 DEV pins are control-points pins (mostly > 5 km) that GT was fitted to. Near bands hold
    one pin each. Pinned acceptance is PENDING the blind interior pins.
  - Evidence conflict: from the GT start the solve drifts 3–6 px from the pins on 5 photos, with any DEM
    and with or without WP-G. Candidates:
    - skyline detection vs DEM horizon on hazy or forested ridges (7018 GT skyline RMS 9 px);
    - WP-G render-match yaw (7018);
    - focal-table pull;
    - notch pins defined on the Terrarium horizon.
    Unresolved.
  - WP-G inliers dominate the near-cue count (e.g. 7131: 1142 points vs 23 contours). They were matched
    from app-eye renders (WP-G known issue) but are used as fixed world points. Forest/building-classed
    points keep σ 4 px.
  - The ultrawide (7059) raw solve is poor: focal σ 4 %, 33 cues. It is blocked there only by LOW
    confidence and the cross-check minimum.
  - Small run-to-run differences (≤ ~0.7 px on single pins). Freshly computed horizons (Float32) and
    disk-cached ones (rounded to 1e-5°) differ slightly, so an LM can land on a slightly different
    optimum. The 0.5–2 km pin read 5.26 in run app-mh and 4.60 in run cues-mh.
  - The eye prior uses WP-B's frozen defaults (altBias −7, σA 3). Per WP-B these were tuned on DEV and
    failed on holdout. Here they sit inside a gated joint solve, never as a standalone snap.
  - k1 never exercised on real data (no corner evidence).
