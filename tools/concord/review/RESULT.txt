FINDINGS
 F1 FIXED  scripts/concord/lib.ts: waterline (level) control-point pins were converted wrongly.
    The old code ray-marched at the hand el, so the ray hit the flat DEM lake plane, not the far shore:
      6971 L/R  289 / 634 m   (labels 450 m / 1 km)   -> now 428 / 892 m
      6958 L/R  295 /  12 m   (label  ~4 km)          -> now 3858 / 3286 m
    The lake level came out as the Terrarium plane (557.0), tied to the GT eye's "2.5 m above water"
    guess. The pins were therefore ~0 px at GT by construction, favoured the GT eye, and 6958 landed in
    the <0.5 km band. Fix: find the far shore along the pixel's azimuth, and take the lake level from
    Mapterhorn over the water (557.66 m). Audit point-pin bands are unchanged (3.74 / 1.68). Side
    finding: 6958's GT eye (GPS 557.2) is below the lake surface, so that GT eye is physically stale.
 F2 FIXED  solve-eval.ts, field-eval.ts, rematch-eval.ts refused holdout entirely. They now run holdout
    only with CONCORD_HOLDOUT=final. field-eval --holdout forbids --sweep and --write-candidates.
    Also fixed "DEV" labels that were hard-coded in the output.
 F3 NOT FIXED (design)  The WP-D gate is too permissive. The last round's cues are matched in a ±12 px
    window around the round-1 camera. Both the gate's cue-RMS test and the 2-fold cue cross-check are
    scored on those cues, so they favour the refined camera, and the cues agree with each other by
    construction. The holdout confirms it: 3/3 non-LOW photos were accepted and 2 got much worse (below).
    7130 moved the eye 200 m (2.9σH against a 3σH limit) with focal z 3.1 (limit 4). Needs a pin
    holdout (ctx.holdout) or a much tighter gate before ?concord=solve is ever wired.
 F4 NOTE  eval.ts --loo builtin:rot keeps photos with ≤2 pins at GT (in-sample, disclosed by WP-A).
    Excluding those 8 pins does not change the dev LOO median (4.57 px, n=26 vs 34).
 F5 NOTE  useConcordDisplay counts alignState "accepted" as high confidence even when the second opinion
    says "kept" or "timeout". This is the app's own accept rule, so it is acceptable. Manual, pinned
    and unverified poses fail closed.
 F6 CHECKED, no defect  The field direction is consistent everywhere (render = photo + W, labels at
    photo = r + W⁻¹(r)). So is the v flip (texelFetch row 0 = top, sampled at (x, 1 − vUv.y), dv
    negated) in both composites. Occluder mask orientation matches the fg mask. The 1600-width pin basis
    matches annotate-lib, and edge cues are converted from predicted to observed by residual·n.
    With the flags off: warpUV returns vUv, the occluder branch is gated, label u/v is unchanged when
    warp = null, geo/camera takes the EXIF path, and the hook returns before any engine call.
    The warp does not reach pose, confidence, GeoJSON (peaksInFrame), sampleAt or pinError.
    No holdout tuning was found: the LENS_TABLE evidence is dev-only and the holdout has no ultrawide.
