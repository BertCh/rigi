1. Photo-level dev / holdout split
----------------------------------
Unit: PHOTO (all pins of a photo share its split). Only the 14 photos with a GT pose in
data/ground-truth.json take part.

Rule (deterministic, reproducible):
  - Strata by region (the photos in a stratum share terrain, lighting, lakes and often an eye):
      thun-shore  : lake-shore photos on Lake Thun (lake in frame, eye 1.6-3 m over water)
      niederhorn  : Niederhorn summit area (Justistal / Stockhorn chain / Bernese Alps)
      stoos       : Stoos / Klingenstock (canton Schwyz)
      non-ch      : outside Switzerland (Maine, La Palma)
  - In each stratum, sort the photos by sha1("<photo>:concord-v1") (hex, ascending).
  - The first max(1, floor(0.3 * n)) photos of each stratum are HOLDOUT; the rest are DEV.
  Result: 10 dev / 4 holdout (29% holdout); every stratum has exactly one holdout photo.
  Lake presence is covered: one of the four lake-shore photos is holdout; 7053 (lake far below,

Photos without a GT pose (IMG_3304, IMG_4527, IMG_4703, IMG_6326, IMG_7108) are not part of

Rules of use:
  - Tune (kernel lengths, sigmas, gate thresholds, which cues to use) on DEV photos only, and on
    dev pins only through leave-one-out (scripts/concord/eval.ts --loo).
  - HOLDOUT photos are scored ONCE per package, for the final report, with the configuration
    frozen beforehand. Do not iterate on holdout numbers. eval.ts refuses --split holdout unless
    CONCORD_HOLDOUT=final is set.
  - Report every number with n and the split it came from. Report failures.
  - Caveat: the control-points.json pins are the constraints the GT poses were fitted to, so at
    the GT pose they are IN-SAMPLE. They measure GT self-consistency, not accuracy. Only the
    blind interior pins (section 2) measure interior concordance.

2. Blind clicking protocol (for the user)
-----------------------------------------
Goal: >= 8 interior pins per photo (>= 3 closer than 2 km where such features are visible), on
>= 10 of the 14 GT photos, including the holdout photos.

What an interior pin is: a small, sharp, identifiable world feature BELOW the skyline whose map
coordinates are known independently of the photo: a church tower base or spire, a cable-car or
rail station building corner, a bridge end, a lake-shore point (harbour mole tip, jetty end, a
distinct shoreline corner), a road/path junction, a hut, a pylon. Not peaks or skyline notches
(those are already covered by control-points.json).

BLIND means: you never see any overlay, projected label, rendered DEM, or predicted position
while clicking. Use only the plain photo and an ordinary map (swisstopo / OSM).

Procedure:
  1. Run:  npx tsx tools/concord/pins/candidates.mts            (all 14 photos, needs network once)
     It writes out/concord/pins/candidates.json (feature names, kinds, map coordinates, a map
     link; NO image positions) and out/concord/pins/click.html (the clicking helper).
     It also writes out/concord/pins/candidates-predicted.json with the GT-pose projections used
     to pre-filter candidates to the frame. DO NOT OPEN that file (or any overlay of it) before
     you have finished clicking a photo.
  2. Open out/concord/pins/click.html in a browser. Pick a photo; the page shows the plain photo
     at the 1600 basis and the candidate list (name, kind, distance, map link). No overlays.
  3. For each candidate: open its map link, find the feature in the photo by your own reading of
     the landscape. If you can identify it confidently to within ~3 px, click it; otherwise press
     "skip". Never guess: a skipped pin costs nothing, a wrong pin poisons the evaluation.
       - Point features (towers, station corners, bridge ends): click the exact ground contact
         point if visible, else the top, and choose "base"/"top" (the helper records hAbove = 0
         for base; for a top, enter the height above ground if known, otherwise skip).
       - Shore points: click where water meets land at the named point; the helper records it as
         kind "shore" with the map coordinates of that point.
       - Waterlines (elevation-only): a click anywhere on the far waterline of a lake whose level
         you know (lake level in m from swisstopo or the BAFU gauge on that day): kind "waterline",
         level.lakeM. (click.html does not create waterline pins; add them by hand.)
     You may add your own features (kind "other") with coordinates read off swisstopo
     (map.geo.admin.ch, right-click → coordinates, height from swissALTI3D).
  4. Aim for spread: left/centre/right, near/far, and at least one pin in a corner region if a
     feature exists there (the corners carry the focal/k1 signal).
  5. Click each photo in one sitting; do not revisit a photo after you have seen any overlay of it.
  6. "Export" in the helper copies JSON; paste the points into tools/concord/pins/interior-pins.json
     ("points" array). Every pin carries the photo's split from section 1 (the helper fills it).
  7. Note in "note" anything uncertain (partly hidden, snow, haze). Set sigmaPx if you think your
     click is worse than 2 px (default).

Pin schema: src/lib/concord/core/types.ts InteriorPin (basis 1600, v down, lat/lon WGS84,
h absolute metres or absent → DEM at lat/lon + hAbove; level.lakeM for waterlines).

3. Evaluation
-------------
  npx tsx scripts/concord/eval.ts --baseline                 (GT pose, dev photos)
  npx tsx scripts/concord/eval.ts --baseline --pose app      (the app's automatic skyline solve)
  npx tsx scripts/concord/eval.ts --candidate out/concord/<pkg>/   (per-photo <photo>.json files)
  npx tsx scripts/concord/eval.ts --loo builtin:rot | <module exporting fitWithout>
Distance bands: <0.5km, 0.5-2km, 2-5km, 5-15km, >15km. Radius bands (fraction of half-diagonal):
centre < 0.35 <= mid < 0.7 <= corner. Pixels at the 1600 basis.
