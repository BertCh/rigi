API vs plan (additive / clarifying only)
  - Signatures of occludingContourCues, matchEdgeCues, waterCues and the GeomBuffer / PhotoEdges shapes
    are as in the plan. Extra optional fields: GeomBuffer.eye, .frame {alt0, rEff}, .cast(u,v) (sub-pixel
    refinement); PhotoEdges.lum. Extra optional options on every function.
  - matchEdgeCues returns an extra `pol` per cue; MatchOpts.polarity accepts "auto".
  - waterCues takes an optional 5th arg opts; with photoWater = null it NEEDS opts.edges (the plan
    signature has no edges argument, so "use photo edges" requires this addition).
  - Lake convention (plan left it open): polygon = SCENE-frame [e, n] metres; levelM = ABSOLUTE altitude.
    GeomBuffer.frame converts it (z = alt − alt0 − d²/2R_eff, the scripts/concord/lib.ts eval frame).
  - Cue conventions: edge normal (nu, nv) is a unit vector in isotropic pixels (x right, y down) pointing
    from the occluder into the farther side; depthM = horizontal distance from the eye (as pin distM);
    residualPx = predicted − observed along the normal, px @1600 (long side 1600). Level/shore cues:
    u, v = OBSERVED pixel; el = target elevation angle (deg) of the lake-level shore point;
    residualPx = f·(el(observed ray) − el(target)), exactly how scorePins scores level pins. Water cues
    also carry lakeM, world (lake-level shore point), predV (predicted row). shoreDist is signed
    (negative inside the water).
  - Deviations from the plan's data sources:
      * tools/research/tm/c0_cache only covers the wild-set wc_* photos, not the 14 GT photos, so
        dump.mts ray-casts the eval scene's Terrarium DEM on the CPU (raycast.ts, 2–3 s per photo at 800
        px). Frame/curvature identical to the eval pins.
      * align.ts buildEdgeMap needs a DOM canvas, so the CPU path re-implements its per-pixel maths;
        photoEdgesFromEdgeMap is the read-only reuse path for the app (type-only import of align.ts).
      * Lakes come from the cached Overpass water queries of WP-A (out/concord/pins/osm/*_water.json:
        natural=water ways + multipolygon relations with member geometry, the region.ts query shape).
        lakesFromOverpass takes region.ts's water elements directly. No network was used.
  - Lake level = median Terrarium DEM inside the polygon (≤ 30 km): Thunersee 557.0 m (real ~557.7–558),
    Vierwaldstättersee 430.0 m (real ~433.6). The Terrarium lake surface is 0.7–3.6 m low.

Method notes / rejections added during dev tuning (dev photos only)
  - Grazing-surface rejection: a lake or flat valley at grazing incidence shows log-range jumps > log 1.3
    between adjacent pixels without any occlusion (IMG_6971: the lake surface near the horizon). A cue is
    kept only if the range jump survives the sub-pixel bisection (ratio > √1.3 at 1/1000 px).
  - Photo clutter: conf = uniqueness × strength × (share of thin edges in the search band that belong to
    the chosen edge); per-photo auto polarity; neighbour-consistency filter (≥ 2 same-kind neighbours
    within 32 px, residual within 2 px of their median). These removed most matches on faces and trees.
  - Mirror-axis waterline (calm-water reflections): implemented but OFF by default. On the 4 dev lake
    photos it was 2–3× rougher along the shore (median 2nd difference 0.8–1.4 vs 0.25–0.7 px) and worse
    against the 6971 hand pins (0.21° vs 0.02°) than the edge search. The edge search sometimes locks on
    the lower rim of a reflection (seen on 7033's right shore).

Known issues
  - SYSTEMATIC BIAS: at the GT pose, edge-cue residuals are negative in every band (median −1.5 to −2.7 px).
    The photo silhouette lies beyond (usually above) the Terrarium DTM silhouette. This is consistent
    with forest canopy on ridges and Terrarium smoothing crests, i.e. DTM vs DSM (the plan's error budget:
    ~4 px on an 8.5 km forested ridge).
    WP-D must not read this as pose. Options: a per-photo edge-cue bias nuisance term, down-weighting
    forested silhouettes, or DSM range from WP-F.
    Water level cues have their own −1 to −4 px offset: observed waterline below predicted. Candidate
    causes are eye height, the 0.7–3.6 m low Terrarium lake level, or reflections.
  - DEM: Terrarium z13 (<4 km) / z11 (<40 km) / z10, matching the eval pins. Near-field contours are
    coarse. GT eye heights are stale by up to +87 m against Mapterhorn on Niederhorn (WP-A). The
    predicted 7053 waterline crosses the foreground cliff for this reason.
  - Search range is ±12 px (edges) / ±10 px (water) at the given pose. Larger pose errors (app pose on
    5495/7155, 160–180 px) produce chance matches, so re-extract after each solver iteration.
  - No person/foreground mask on the CPU path (see audit). Reflections can bias waterlines by a few px.
  - Photos without GPS-lake overlap (5495 in the US; 7131/7155, where Vierwaldstättersee is out of view)
    get no water cues. 7155 has no WP-A OSM cache of its own; the merged cache covered its lake.
  - Interior pins clicked by the user do not exist yet (WP-A PENDING). The sign test therefore used only
    the 34 control-point dev pins, mostly far summits.
