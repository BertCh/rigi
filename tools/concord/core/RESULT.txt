API notes (additive only; nothing removed or renamed)
  - Intrinsics.k1 convention: distorted = ideal·(1 + k1·r²) in focal-normalised coords (the audit's
    convention, where pins favour k1 ≈ +0.04). The plan comment said "division model"; the plan also
    specified an iterative undistort, which only makes sense for this forward-polynomial model.
  - undistortUV uses 5 Newton iterations on the radius, not the plain fixed point: the plain
    fixed point reached only 2.3e-4 uv at wide-FOV corners, which fails the 1e-6 acceptance.
  - Additive exports: DISTANCE_BANDS, RADIUS_BANDS, BandStat, radiusFrac, sampleFieldSigma,
    decodeFieldRGBA8. invertField defaults to 8 iterations.
  - RGBA8 encoding: R,G = du hi,lo; B,A = dv hi,lo; value = (code/65535·2−1)·scale.
  - Pin basis: x,y are in the 1600-WIDTH basis (the control-points.json/engine.ts convention;
    portrait = 1600×2133). Residual px are reported at the long-side-1600 basis (the audit's "px @1600").
  - Scene frame for eval: ENU at the GT lat/lon, z = alt − GT eye with the d²/(2R_eff) drop folded in.
    GT cam has eye [0,0,0]; candidates move the eye via cam.eye.
  - Radius band = hypot((u−.5)·aspect, v−.5) / half-diagonal (the audit's rnorm was a different norm).

  baselineCam(photo, "gt"|"app"): "gt" (default) = GT pose + GT eye from ground-truth.json (the pose the

  Candidate file format: {cam: CameraX, field?: ResidualField (arrays OK)}. The field is applied as

Known issues
  - Overpass main was overloaded (504/429) during the run:
      * 7131 and 7155 got no candidates.
      * The bridge/pier ("lines") or shore ("water") queries failed for the 6958, 7068, 7086, 7130
        and Niederhorn locations.
      * Failed queries are not cached; rerun candidates.mts later to fill them in (successful ones are cached).
  - Summit photos looking at far scenery (6019, 7068, 7086, and 7053 partly) have 0–7 in-frame
    features within 0.3–5 km, so ≥ 8 pins may be impossible there. The candidate check uses Terrarium
    line of sight: no trees or buildings, and coarse near the eye.
  - click.html records point pins only; waterline pins are added by hand. localStorage keeps clicks per browser.
  - Mapterhorn eye-ground tiles are fetched once into .cache/dem-mapterhorn. CONCORD_OFFLINE=1 skips them.
  - The control-points pins are the constraints the GT was fitted to, so GT-pose numbers on them are in-sample.
