API vs plan (additive only)
  - EyePrior gets extra fields: g0, mapEye (the prior's MAP eye), reason, bandFrac. Plan fields are unchanged.
  - eyePriorFromExif(meta, ground, opts?): opts is an optional 3rd parameter (EYE_PRIOR_DEFAULTS).
  - altitudeContourCost(eye, p, eyeAboveGround = 1.6) and isoBandSeeds(p, n = 12, eyeAboveGround = 1.6):
    optional trailing parameters.
  - LensEntry adds optional f35 (the entry applies only at that EXIF 35 mm focal) and evidence.
  - focalPrior(lensModel, f35, px, sensor?, source?): optional crop pass-through to focalPxFromF35. It returns
    {fPx, sigmaPx} plus fScale and entry.
  - nearGround(lat, lon, opts?): opts {radiusM, zoom, signal}. It uses a dynamic import so node checks don't
    load deck/**. In node, loadDemTile (createImageBitmap) is unavailable, so the study builds the same GroundFn
    from Mapterhorn tiles through groundFromHeightAt.
  - New exports: concordEye(meta, ground, flags) → {alt, lat, lon, dE, dN, shiftM, prior} | null, and
    refineEyeOptions(p) for pose6dof/eye.ts.
  - Frame: ENU at the GPS fix, z in m MSL. ground(dE, dN) is the DEM at an offset from the fix, the eye.ts
    convention.

1. MSL-vs-ellipsoid consistency (all 19 GPS photos; npx tsx scripts/concord/priors-study.ts consistency)
   DEM: Mapterhorn at the finest zoom with data (z17 in CH, z15/16 elsewhere).
   r = alt − (DEM(fix) + 1.6).
   N is an approximate EGM2008 undulation, hand-entered per region (±3 m). It is not a model evaluation: there is
   no geoid model in the repo and downloads are out of scope. The two readings differ by |N| ≥ 16 m everywhere,
   so the classification does not depend on N's precision.
   "disk σ / 2σ" = the range of DEM + 1.6 − alt within hAcc / 2·hAcc of the fix.
   "iso" = the distance to the nearest point where |DEM + 1.6 − alt| ≤ 2 m (MSL reading) or ≤ 2 m of alt − N
   (ellipsoidal reading).

   Verdict: GPSAltitude is MSL (Apple: EGM2008), not ellipsoidal.
   - The ellipsoidal reading puts the eye 33–70 m below every DEM point within 2·hAcc on 15 of 19 photos.
   - It survives only where the 2σ disk is huge (7059: hAcc 37 m over a cliff; 7130: hAcc 70 m).
   - Every photo is consistent with MSL once horizontal error is allowed (iso-band within 0–59 m, ≤ 1.8σH).
     The exceptions are the flat lake/cab cases, which read 3–8 m low.
   - In CH the residual is mostly negative (−3 to −20 m), which is where the dev bias fit below comes from.

   The three outliers explained:
   - IMG_7155 (−18.5 m). The fix sits on the Klingenstock crest. Mapterhorn gives 1934.7 m there, which is the
     summit height, and the slope at the fix is 2°. The photo looks SW (yaw 236°) and down (pitch −8.7°).
     - The GPS-altitude contour lies 16 m from the fix at azimuth 235°: straight ahead, down the SW slope. That is
       1.0σH (hAcc 16.5 m).
     - Reading: the photographer stood about 10–16 m below the crest on the SW-facing slope, and the fix landed on
       the crest. The GPS altitude is right.
     - The old +35 m "GPS above ground" statement in ground-truth.json comes from the Terrarium DEM, which is
       52 m low at this fix. It is not a GPS error.
   - IMG_7131 (+13.0 m). The fix is on a 23° slope with hAcc 41 m. Ground at alt − 1.6 lies 26 m away (0.6σH),
     up-slope to the SW, behind a camera that looks N.
     - Reading: a 26 m horizontal error on a 23° slope (≈ 11 m of height), or a raised platform in Stoos. Both are
       consistent with MSL; the data can't separate them.
   - IMG_7130 (+15.5 m). This is the worst fix in the set: hAcc 70 m, and the fix is 3.5 s older than the shutter.
     The slope at the fix is 9°. The band is 51–59 m away (0.8σH).
     - Reading: horizontal error on the Stoos village slope, possibly on a building (village; buildings are not in
       the DTM).
     - Being above ground here is not evidence of an ellipsoidal reading (ellipsoidal would be −33 m, which fits
       no other Stoos photo).
   - The same-day Stoos pair 7130/7131 reads high because both fixes landed down-slope. The other CH photos read
     low because photographers stand at lips and edges, and the band then lies in the viewing direction:
     7155 235° vs yaw 236°, 7033 212° vs 218°, 7053 274° vs 258°, 7018 200° vs 160°.
     This view-direction pattern would be a useful term for WP-D (band side ≈ compass heading), but it is not
     implemented: n is small.

2. Altitude noise σA (DEV photos only; priors-study sigma)
   Model: marginal likelihood of alt = DEM(x) + 1.6 + b + N(0, σA²), with x ~ N(fix, hAcc²).
   - bias 0: MLE σA = 8 m. The curve is flat from 5 to 12. Leave-one-photo-out gives 6–8.
   - Joint fit: b = −7 m, σA = 3 m, ΣlogL −34.08 vs −37.94. LR = 7.7 for one extra parameter.
   - Robustness check, DEV + the 5 no-GT photos (--with-nogt): b = −4, σA = 3 (LR 8.8).
   - Same-spot pair 7059/7063 (13 s apart; tools/nearfield/eyes shows the baseline is < 1 m): Δalt 4.45 m, so the
     per-fix σ is about 3.1 m. This is consistent with σA = 3.
   Frozen defaults (EYE_PRIOR_DEFAULTS): sigmaA 3, altBias −7, σH = clamp(hAcc, 5, 100), fallback σV 50.
   - This is the joint MLE. It was also the only one of the pre-listed variants that passed the dev skyline guard
     (section 4).
   - Caveat: 9 of the 10 DEV photos are Swiss, from 4 days in one week. The US no-GT fixes read b ≈ 0.

4. Eye rule on DEV photos (priors-study eye; also scripts/concord/eval.ts --candidate)
   Method, per photo:
   - Eye by rule, on the same DEM (Mapterhorn: z17 within 300 m, then the app's MAPTERHORN levels).
   - computeHorizon at that eye, including its lat/lon shift.
   - fitRotationToHorizon (pose6dof/eye.ts) on the detectSkyline samples, starting from the GT pose with a
     10° rotation prior; vfov at the GT f.
   - Score every control-point pin with the WP-A eval lib. Bands use the pin's distance from the GT eye, so every
     rule is scored on the same bands.
   The pins are not used by the fit, so they are out-of-sample for all rules. They are mostly > 5 km.

   Per protocol, nothing was re-tuned after this. Untested suggestions for any re-registration: cap the MAP shift
   (e.g. ≤ 1σH or 30 m), altBias 0 outside a calibrated region, and use the prior only inside a joint solve (WP-D),
   never as a standalone MAP eye.

Known issues
  - Pins: every DEV/holdout pin is a control-points.json pin, mostly > 5 km, where eye position barely matters.
    The interior-pin acceptance is pending the user's blind clicks.
  - GT eyes are Terrarium-era, 44–87 m off on Niederhorn/Stoos. The study compares rules on one DEM (Mapterhorn),
    but notch and waterline pin coordinates were built from the GT eye on Terrarium (WP-A lib), which biases
    near/level pins towards the GT eye.
  - Geoid N values are approximate constants per region, not EGM2008 evaluations.
  - The altBias −7 m is a regional/temporal effect. It could be barometric drift or the "stand at the lip" effect
    in section 1, and it hurt on holdout. Keep it out of any default-on path.
  - Mapterhorn tiles fetched: +58 MB in .cache/dem-mapterhorn (65 MB total). Horizon cache: out/concord/priors/cache (12 MB).
