API DEVIATIONS (all additive)
  - loadNearDsm(lat, lon, radiusM = 2000, resM = 2, opts?): opts = { fetcher, json, signal, wedge {yawDeg, halfDeg},
    dtmRes 4|2 (default 4 = swissALTI3D 4 m overview), maxTiles, maxBytes (default 4.5e6) }.
    Default maxBytes means a 2 km radius is not always fully covered: km tiles are kept nearest-first while the
    planned DSM + DTM bytes fit. Pass maxBytes: Infinity for full coverage.
  - NearDsm has extra fields: e0, n0 (ENU of the centre of cell (0,0), the NW corner), radiusM, stats
    {requests, bytes, stacRequests, stacBytes, ms, fetchMs, tiles, dtmRes}, years {dsm[], dtm[]}.
    Grid: cell (i,j) sits at e = e0 + i*res, n = n0 - j*res. Heights are absolute (not curvature-dropped).
    Frame = EnuFrame(lat, lon, 0), which is the engine's frame.
  - occluderRange(g, cam, dsm, objects?, people?, opts?): opts {minObjM 2.5, nearSkipM 15, stepFrac 0.5, eyeZ}.
    cam.eye must be engine world coordinates [0, 0, eyeAlt]. Output rows run top to bottom, in metres;
    Infinity means nothing occludes; people -> 0.
  - GeomBuffer is declared locally in occluder.ts with WP-C's exact shape (WP-C's cues package does not exist yet).
    The integration agent can switch it to an import.

DATA / TRANSPORT FACTS, verified today with real requests
  - STAC: data.geo.admin.ch/api/stac/v0.9. Collections ch.swisstopo.swisssurface3d-raster (0.5 m DSM, one year
    per km tile, 2019-2024 seen) and ch.swisstopo.swissalti3d (0.5 m and 2 m assets; several editions per tile,
    e.g. 2019 + 2025. The newest is used). Responses carry Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *.
  - COG range reads: GET with Range returns 206 and ACAO *. The OPTIONS preflight for the range header returns
    204 with access-control-allow-headers: range. So the reads work from the browser as-is.
  - swissSURFACE3D COG: 2000x2000 float32, LZW, predictor 1 (so essentially uncompressed), 512² tiles,
    overviews at 1 m (1000²) and 2 m (500²), nodata -9999, LV95.
    The 2 m level of one km tile is a single 512² tile of about 1.0-1.1 MB, so it cannot be partially read.
    Measured cost: ~1 MB per km² at 2 m, ~4 MB per km² at 1 m.
  - swissALTI3D 2 m COG: 500² float32 LZW, 128² tiles, 4 m overview. The overview costs ~0.26 MB/km²;
    the full 2 m level costs ~1 MB/km².
  - Decoder verified on a real tile: min/max/mean 862.502 / 1713.922 / 1255.831 m. These match the file's own
    GDAL statistics metadata exactly.
  - DSM overview vs DTM grids align: open ground gives nDSM ≈ 0 (q25 0.01 m with the 2 m DTM, -0.30 m with the
    4 m DTM). Epoch mismatch is real: DSM 2019-2024 vs DTM 2025/2026 on the same tiles.

KNOWN ISSUES
  - The 2 m DSM level is one ~1 MB tile per km², so byte cost is quantised per km tile. Under the 4.5 MB budget
    typically 3 tiles are covered, nearest first. Distant forest silhouettes beyond them are not occluders.
  - Swiss only. Outside CH it returns null; CHMv2 / OSM buildings are Phase 2.
  - Inherits the pose and eye errors: see IMG_7131 (GPS altitude 15 m high).
  - Epoch mismatch: DSM 2019-2024 vs DTM 2025/26. Felled or new trees and huts are wrong by up to 5-7 years.
  - The LV95 approximate formulas are good to ~1 m. That is negligible against GPS error, but not survey grade.
  - Latency tail (CloudFront miss) 2-7 s from this machine. The integration should load asynchronously and
    apply the texture when ready, never blocking first paint.
  - occluderRange is 170-225 ms at 320x240 on CPU. Use 160x120 or a worker.
  - Cache: tools/concord/occl/cache holds 36 MB (product config for the dev photos + the DTM 2 m variant).
    Delete it freely; the scripts refetch.
