WHAT WAS RUN

  RING (24 yaws at the stated eye), YAW (+-6/+-12 deg about the correct ref), DISPLACED eyes. Every hypothesis gets one
  LoMa single-view correspondence set; T(h) = min over 4 held-out directions (far->near, near->far, L->R, R->L) of a
  binomial hit score; per-photo label-free null over the photo's other hypotheses; NFA = N_tests x Gaussian tail.
  Scale-down (D1, pre-declared in the protocol, written before any statistic): displaced eyes cut to bearing theta x
  {150, 400} m for all photos (the ~4 h session limit and the render/gpu locks shared with E2/E3 could not fit the full

  All 35 photos were completed; none was dropped. Infrastructure (D2): renders used a private Vite server on :3196
  (the pre-crash run used the default :3100, which the fund rules forbid). Photos 1-5 were rendered before the crash,

DESCRIPTIVE (not decisions)
  AUROC POS vs kind of the held-out statistic T: NB-inh 0.89, NB-con 0.89, NB-dec 0.89, NE-inh 0.71, NE-dec 0.82.
  The no-held-out Tfit: NB-inh 0.84, NB-con 0.98, NB-dec 1.00, NE-inh 0.63, NE-dec 0.87.
  So the held-out split does not buy wrong-eye separation. Tfit separates the displaced decoys better than T does.
  Both are weakest on the eye kinds, which is the parallax/observability problem E3 targets.
  In 10 of 29 P_ref photos, a >= 150 m decoy has T >= the photo's best positive. Five of those are ties at T = 0.

WHY IT FAILED (POST HOC analysis - mechanisms seen after scoring; not pre-registered)
  1. Degenerate null. 90% of all hypotheses have T = 0: ring views mostly have almost no matches, and many held-out
     directions score k = 0. The per-photo null is mostly zeros. Its sd is small (median 0.56 in z units, at the
     0.05 floor for 21% of hypotheses), so any hypothesis with modest agreement gets a huge Gaussian z. log10 NFA runs
     to -895. The Gaussian tail says nothing here; the protocol's HONESTY note flagged this risk.
     The empirical P, (1 + #{z >= z_h}) / (1 + |N|), is >= 1/(1+~30) ~ 0.03 for every hypothesis. With N_tests ~ 32,
     an empirical NFA can never be < 1, so a calibrated version with these null sizes could accept nothing at all.
  2. A min over 4 directions is brittle for correct poses. 7 of 33 positives have T = 0 despite 800-1900 matches and
     large Tfit (e.g. wc_0009_k000: Tfit 4101, T 0). A rotation-only refit on one half (usually the near half, or one
     image side) drifts ~0.4-1.3 deg, and the held-out half then gets k = 0 hits within 3 sigma (~10 px). The
     direction that zeroes T is most often A->B (240 of the 374 hypotheses with >= 6 matches and T = 0).
  3. Wrong eyes fit the held-out cue as well as right ones. A 150-400 m eye shift followed by a rotation re-solve leaves
     structured misfit, but when both halves are dominated by distant terrain, far->near and L/R agreement stays high.
     Held-out agreement does not measure parallax.
  Oracle thresholds (POST HOC, chosen with labels, so an upper bound on the idea with this statistic): the best photo
  recall at 0 gross is 12/31 on log10 NFA (the cut is set by NE-inh wc_0086_k007 at -39.3), 10/31 on raw T, 12/31 on
  Tfit, and 9/31 on the fit-NFA. All are below CUR's 19/31. Even a label-tuned cut on this statistic does not reach the
  current rule at 0 gross.

HONESTY / CAVEATS
  - n is small: 31 photos with positives, 5 inherited wrong-eye candidates, 56 displaced decoys from 29 photos. The CIs
    above are by photo. Single cases are anecdotes.
  - D1 cut: 2 displaced eyes per photo instead of 9. More decoys could only add wrong-eye accepts (the kill is already
    hit), so the cut cannot rescue the verdict.
  - "Wrong by construction" for 150 m relies on the parallax argument in PROTOCOL.txt. The misfit diagnostic shows a few
    near misses; see the post hoc relabelling above, which leaves the verdict unchanged.
  - The app code used for photos 6-35 may differ slightly from photos 1-5 (D2). Not tested.
  - The current rule's 0 gross on NE-dec partly reflects its conservative CUR definition: moved eyes are never
    auto-accepted, and displaced hypotheses are excluded by construction. CUR-all, the gate stack applied to the
    displaced solves, accepts 9/56. That finding is interesting in itself: rule.high on its own would accept wrong
    eyes. It is outside the E1 kill.

IMPLICATION FOR THE PLAN (interpretation)
  E1 as specified should not replace the T6 gate or absorb R1/R2. The held-out-cue statistic does not separate wrong eyes
  from right ones, and the per-photo decoy null is too small and too degenerate for NFA calibration. If an a-contrario
  gate is revisited, it needs a larger continuous null, for example many more decoys per photo or pooling across
  photos, with an explicit leave-photo-out step. It also needs a statistic that is sensitive to parallax (near/far
  differential residual at a fixed eye) rather than to halves agreeing. E3's near-field renders are the natural input.
