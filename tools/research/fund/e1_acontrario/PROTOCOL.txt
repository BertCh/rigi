HYPOTHESES (per analysed photo; every hypothesis = pose + eye + ONE LoMa-B single-view correspondence set)
- Evidence type everywhere = the H1 "common support" procedure: render ONE sat view at the hypothesis pose and eye
  (worker, 1024 long side, photo aspect), LoMa-B (4096 kp, MPS fp32, filter 0.1) photo<->view via correspond_loma,
  service lift (>250 m, no depth discontinuity). -> x2d (render px), X (world), eye, W, H.
  (a) POOL: every H1 pool candidate of the photo with a scored single corr (pool.json common.corrSingle; reused, not
      re-matched).
  (b) REF: every refs.correct_refs pose with no pool hypothesis within 0.5 deg yaw & pitch and 2 m eye -> rendered here.
  (c) RING (null generator, stated eye): 24 views yaw 0,15..345, pitch 0, roll 0, hfov 40 deg, rendered at the stated
      eye; LoMa per view; single-view rotation solve (s1.solve, prior = view yaw / pitch 0 / roll 0 / photo vfov0,
      free focal iff focal unknown - the X3/stage-1 single-view solve). Hypothesis pose = solved pose. If the solve
      has >= 30 inliers the evidence is re-made by the common procedure at the solved pose; otherwise the ring view's
      own matches are the evidence (pose = solved pose, or the view pose if the solve failed).
  (d) YAW (P_ref only, null generator): seeds = first correct ref pose with yaw +-6 and +-12 deg; render at the seed,
      LoMa, single-view solve (prior = seed); evidence as in (c).
  (e) DISPLACED EYE (all 35 photos): eyes at distance {50, 150, 400} m x bearings {theta, theta+120, theta+240},
      theta ~ U(0,360) from numpy default_rng(20260929 + int(pid digits)); eye height = DEM ground (tools/matcher/dem.py)
      + 1.7 m (adhoc altitudeM = round(., 2); the worker clamps to its DEM + 1.6 m). Rejected if the DEM has no value.
      Re-solve with the SAME solver the T6/T6+LoMa candidates used: pipeline.stage2 (5-view fan, APP.assemble fused
      solve with skyline + LoMa matches) at the displaced eye with prior2 = the first correct ref pose (P_ref) or, for
      P_lab, the photo's own T6 ALIKED stated-eye rule.select pose (label-free). Hypothesis = the fused pose at the
      achieved eye; evidence re-made by the common procedure at that pose/eye. The stage-2 result (level, checks) is
      stored, and pipeline.basin_gap is computed exactly when run_photo would (level high or matchSupport >= 0.5, and
      positionSource != exif-gps) so the current rule can be applied to it (positionSource = the photo's own).
- Dedupe of generated hypotheses (c-e): a generated hypothesis within 0.5 deg yaw & pitch and 2 m eye of an earlier one
  (order a, b, e, c, d) is dropped.
- Skyline cue (pose-free, label-free): photo edge maps (fine, fg, rgb) from tools/matcher/v2/.cache/edges/<pid>.npz,

  horizon (eye-only); other eyes (H1 S3 / v2 moved eyes) -> one worker `edges` call per eye. If unavailable, the test
  runs on matches only (flagged).

- POSITIVE: a POOL or REF hypothesis within 0.5 deg yaw & pitch and 2 m eye of a pose in V_p.

    NB-con   any other POOL hypothesis within 2 m eye of a V_p pose and > 3 deg in yaw OR pitch from every V_p pose at
             that eye (H1 construction rule, applied to all scored pool candidates; sub-counts: hard = common >= 100 or
             native fused >= 100; S5-sourced).
    NB-dec   RING / YAW hypotheses in P_ref photos, > 3 deg (yaw or pitch) from every V_p pose at the stated eye.
- NEGATIVE, wrong-eye:
    NE-inh   H1 inherited-wrong candidates not in NB-inh (eye not within 2 m of any verified-correct pose; expected
             wc_0001, 0069, 0070, 0074, 0086) - wrong eye presumed, not constructed.
    NE-dec   DISPLACED hypotheses at >= 150 m in P_ref photos (wrong by construction; argument below).
- AMBIGUOUS: DISPLACED at 50 m in P_ref (near-miss; not counted as gross or correct).
- UNLABELLED (excluded from every metric; distributions reported, clearly marked): H1 blind-to-verify candidates, all
  other pool candidates not labelled above (moved-eye, near-correct 0.5-3 deg variants, photos without truth),
  RING/YAW within 3 deg of V_p, all hypotheses of P_lab photos except NE-inh/NB-inh, DISPLACED in P_lab.
- Why >= 150 m is wrong by construction: angular parallax between features at d1 < d2 from an eye error b is
  b (1/d1 - 1/d2). A rotation re-solve absorbs the common shift, not the differential. 150 m: 5 km vs 30 km -> 1.43 deg,
  20 km vs 60 km -> 0.29 deg; at the dev pixel scales (hfov 12-113 deg on 1024 px, i.e. 9-85 px/deg) this is >= ~10 px
  of structured misfit in every photo that has two depth layers within 60 km - a visible near-miss, which the product's
  verification protocol calls wrong. 50 m gives a third of it (a few px on wide frames): ambiguous. As a diagnostic
  (not a label), each displaced hypothesis in P_ref gets the truth-geometry misfit: the correct ref's cached xyz
  (C0 cache refs/<label>/xyz.npz, terrain samples) projected from the displaced eye with the hypothesis rotation vs
  their pixel centres (median / p90 px, native).

STATISTIC  T(h)  (held-out cue agreement; all constants fixed a priori, none fitted to labels)
- Split 1 (depth): A = matches with |X - eye| >= 5 km, plus the skyline cue; B = matches < 5 km. If |A| < 12 or
  |B| < 12 matches, split at the median match depth instead (skyline stays with the far half; flagged "median").
- Split 2 (image halves): L = matches with u < W/2 plus skyline columns with u < W/2; R = the rest.
- Directed test F -> G (4 tests: A->B, B->A, L->R, R->L): rotation-only refit (yaw, pitch, roll; focal fixed at h's vfov)
  on F starting at h's pose: fusion.py's schedule (match gates 30/15/8/8 px, skyline association windows 24/12/6/6 edge
  rows, Huber 1.345 IRLS, per-term MAD sigma floored at 1 px, scipy LM). Fit fails if the last iteration has < 6 gated
  matches and < 10 skyline columns -> s = 0.
  sigma_F = final MAD sigma of F's gated match residuals (per coordinate; skyline sigma if F has no gated matches),
  floor 1 px. tau = 3 sigma_F. For G's matches: e_i = |residual| under the F-fit pose; n = |G|, k = #{e_i <= tau},
  p0 = min(1, pi tau^2 / (W H)) (uniform-background chance of a hit).
  s(F->G) = -log10 P[Binom(n, p0) >= k]  (0 if k = 0 or n = 0).
- T(h) = min of the 4 directed s (every held-out direction must agree). Hypotheses with < 6 matches: T = 0.
- Diagnostic only (pre-registered, not a decision): T_fit = same binomial score of ALL matches under a fit on all
  matches + skyline (no held-out) -> shows what the held-out split adds.

NULL, NFA, DECISION
- Alternatives of h, N(h) (label-free, relative to h): every other hypothesis of the same photo whose eye is >= 100 m
  from h's eye, OR within 2 m of h's eye and > 3 deg (yaw or pitch) from h. Hypotheses 2-100 m from h's eye and
  same-eye ones within 3 deg are excluded from N(h). No label is used to build any null.
- z = log10(1 + T). P_null(h) = 1 - Phi((z_h - mu) / sd), mu / sd = mean / sample sd of z over N(h) (sd floored at
  0.05). If |N(h)| < 10: no decision (reject), flagged. Also reported: the empirical P = (1 + #{z >= z_h}) / (1 + |N(h)|).
- N_tests(p) = number of hypotheses of photo p after dedupe (all kinds). NFA(h) = N_tests(p) x P_null(h).
- PRIMARY accept: log10 NFA < 0 (a-contrario default, eps = 1).
  ALTERNATIVE (pre-registered, the only one): log10 NFA < -2 (eps = 0.01; <= ~0.35 expected false accepts over the
  35 photos if the Gaussian tail were exact).
- Leave-one-photo-out: nothing is fitted across photos (the null is per photo, label-free; all constants fixed above),
  so every decision is already out-of-photo. Stated, not simulated.

METRICS (reported for PRIMARY, ALTERNATIVE, CUR, CUR-all; and the T_fit diagnostic under the same NFA machinery)
- Per kind: accepted / n for POSITIVE, NB-inh, NB-con (hard / easy / S5), NB-dec, NE-inh, NE-dec, AMBIGUOUS; unlabelled
  accept counts and T distributions, marked unlabelled.
- Photo level: recall = # P_ref+P_lab photos with >= 1 POSITIVE accepted; gross = # labelled negatives accepted
  (any kind) and # photos with one.
- "Recall at 0 gross": a method is at 0 gross iff it accepts no labelled negative of any kind. Bootstrap by photo
  (2000 resamples, seed 20260929) 95% CIs for recall and gross; exact binomial upper bound for the per-kind miss rate.
- KILL (per plan), evaluated for PRIMARY and, separately, ALTERNATIVE:
    (i) any NE-dec (displaced >= 150 m, P_ref) accepted, or any NE-inh accepted  -> kill ("wrong-eye decoy accepted");
   (ii) not at 0 gross, or photo recall < CUR's photo recall on the same photos  -> kill.
  E1 passes iff PRIMARY survives both. If only ALTERNATIVE survives: reported as "passes at the pre-registered
  alternative eps only". Anything decided after seeing statistics is labelled POST HOC in REPORT.txt.
