WHAT THIS MEANS
  - The hypothesis's mechanism fails at its first step: correspondences < 250 m essentially do not exist with
    ALIKED+LightGlue against ortho-draped renders, however high-res the DTM/DSM/ortho (21 near inliers in 30 photos).
    Hi-res mid-field rendering gives a noisy, non-significant change in inlier counts.
  - Eye position from parallax works only on the minority of photos with dense mid-band matches (3-4/30), and the
    app-grade (3.3 m DEM, z16 ortho) render already achieves it there.
  - The frozen solver design (far5 rotation, fixed ref rotation for the displaced render) turns many photos into
    automatic failures; a redesigned solver would lift the post-hoc numbers a little, but the G50h ceiling (20-23 %)
    and the (a) null make a pass at 60 % implausible.

LIMITATIONS
  - T is not surveyed; errors include T's own error (wc_0094/0088 suggest T is off by 15-30 m on some photos).
  - Displaced eyes use the ref rotation for the render and fixed intrinsics; a real pipeline would re-render after
    each update. LoMa secondary not run (optional; never verdict-relevant).
  - n = 30; CIs are wide, but the primary point value (10 %) is far from the 60 % bar.
