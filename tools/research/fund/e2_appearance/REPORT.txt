SET AND METHOD (short)
- The 30 dev photos with a correct ref and a perturb base (full planned set, not a subset). Views from the C0 cache:
  every correct ref, every wrong ref, and perturb yaw -8/-4/+4/+8. No new renders, so no render lock was needed.
- Variants are offline post-processing of the cached ortho render. True and decoy views get identical processing.
    (i)   v0  current render.
    (ii)  v1  re-lit: the baked ortho shading (fitted on ring views) is divided out, then multiplied by the photo-time
              sun (Lambert + DEM cast shadow, ambient by weather tag).
    (iii) v2  v1 + a date-matched snow fraction mixing albedo toward snow. Copernicus GFSC had no anonymous access, so
              the snow comes from S2 SCL (+-10 d) and Landsat QA (+-16 d) composites on a ~60 m grid.
    (iv)  v5  v2 shading + the nearest clear S2 L2A true colour (low frequencies from S2, high frequencies from the
              ortho). The source is Earth Search, with Planetary Computer as fallback before 2018. It applies to 11
              photos (date >= 2016-11 and >= 50% S2 cover of the true view).
    Extras (not plan variants, not in the verdict): v3 = v2 + a per-view haze/gain fit to the photo; v4 = v0 + the
    same fit.
- LoMa-B (4096 kp, MPS fp32) and ALIKED+LightGlue. Photo LANCZOS-resized to the view. X3 lifting (>250 m, no depth
  discontinuity) and X3 eval_view. v0 LoMa matches reproduce X3's cached 'sat' matches exactly (checked on 3 wc_0002
  views: identical keypoints).
- I = cons6 at the true (perturb-base) ref = lifted matches within 6 px of the true pose, no solve.
  Gain = (I_v - I_0) / max(I_0, 10), median over photos.
  D = max cons6 over the decoys (wrong refs + perturbs, each at its own pose). S = log10((I+1)/(D+1)).
  Separation gain = median dS > 0 AND a one-sided Wilcoxon p < 0.05.

WHAT THE NUMBERS SAY
1. Sun relighting (ii) does nothing measurable. It is +-1% overall and +3-6% in the low-sun group, with individual
   photos going both ways (wc_0099: LoMa -24%, ALIKED +28%). The matchers are already largely invariant to shading
   direction at these view scales, or the Lambert + cast-shadow model is too crude to help.
2. The snow tint (iii), as implemented, HURTS exactly where it acts. Snowy photos lose 31% (LoMa) and 52% (ALIKED) of
   true-pose inliers. Worst cases: wc_0048 1024 -> 341, wc_0059 747 -> 73, wc_0067 1141 -> 486; ALIKED drops to 54, 0
   and 1. One snowy photo gains: wc_0020, +14% LoMa, +12% ALIKED.
   POST HOC diagnosis from examples/wc_0048__refs__B.jpg: the mix replaces textured ortho with a flat snow albedo and,
   at 60 m, paints snow over dark cliffs that are bare in the photo. This removes the high-frequency texture the
   matchers use. This finding is about the implementation, not proof that snow physics cannot help: a
   texture-preserving tint with slope/aspect masking was not tested.
3. Nearest clear S2 colour (iv) is neutral overall (-0.2% / 0.0%, n = 11) and mixed per photo. wc_0014 gains (LoMa
   972 -> 1129, ALIKED 713 -> 833). wc_0047 ALIKED and wc_0011 LoMa lose about 10-17%. On snowy photos it is
   harmless where (iii) was harmful (wc_0048 LoMa 934 vs v2's 341). Real on-date snow keeps texture, so it does
   not show the flat-tint failure. Too few photos to claim more.
4. Photo-fitted haze/gain (extras v3/v4) is clearly harmful: -19 to -25% median, 22-25 of 30 photos worse. Fitting the
   photo's colour and airlight onto the render degrades matching. This agrees with the X3 finding that generic
   post-processing does not help, and is stronger.
5. Separation. The decoys already score ~0 on 27-28 of 30 photos (median D = 0, both matchers). No variant flipped
   any of the photos where a decoy beats the truth: wc_0002 (LoMa I 6 vs D 163) and wc_0028 (9 vs 181), plus wc_0006
   on ALIKED. These are the wrong-basin cases E1 lists as inherited NB. Their true-pose I stays at 4-9 under every
   variant. Appearance is not what separates them.

CAVEATS (honest)
- The separation half is weak as a test. The simple decoys (wrong refs + yaw +-4/8 perturbs, scored by
  pose-consistency) are nearly all at 0 at baseline, so there is little room to improve, and only 2-3 photos carry
  information. The plan's E1-score separation is PENDING: it needs E1's a-contrario statistic on E1's hypotheses,
  which requires fresh renders and skyline cues.
- Structural: a median-over-30-photos rule cannot be passed by a change that alters only a minority of photos. Snow
  acts on 6 photos, S2 exists for 11. The criterion is fixed and applied as written. The subgroup numbers above are
  the fairer read for snow and S2, and they are also negative, or n = 3.
- Deviations from the plan text:
  (a) GFSC was replaced by an S2-SCL/Landsat snow composite (no anonymous GFSC access on 2026-09-29).
  (b) Snow coverage is incomplete. No usable data for wc_0019, 0027, 0028, 0055, 0063, 0072. Winter photos without
      snow data (wc_0072, 0085, 0099) got no snow tint.
  (c) S2 before 2018 came from Planetary Computer (Earth Search has no pre-2018 items over the Alps).
  (d) (iv) covers 11 photos. wc_0009 was excluded (true-view S2 cover 0.10); wc_0054 had no clear scene.
- Capture times are EXIF DateTimeOriginal read as Europe/Zurich time. Camera clocks are unverified, and a wrong clock
  shifts the sun used in (ii).
- n = 30 dev photos, one ref per photo. Per-photo effects are anecdotes.
