SIGMA_PRED: median 0.013 deg, range 0.002-0.045 deg; the true error exceeds 3 sigma on 102/102 runs (median error /
  sigma = 213). The Gauss-Newton covariance is wildly over-confident (patch tokens overlap and the residual is
  dominated by a modelling floor, not noise), so even a rank correlation of 0.39 is not a usable covariance.

POST HOC (labelled; never used for the verdict)
  - The objective does not prefer the truth: the refined cost is below the cost at the GT pose in 92 % of runs
    (94/102). The cost surface is nearly flat (~0.15-0.19 for start, GT and refined alike): photo and
    render tokens differ by ~0.9 in unit-vector distance almost everywhere (orthophoto-draped render vs a photo with
    foreground, clouds, light). The LM follows the optimiser's noise, f drifts (-30 % ... +4 %) and takes rotation
    with it. This is an objective problem, not an optimiser one (the synthetic test converges).
  - Spearman by set: primary only 0.52, secondary only 0.35 (n = 12 and 90).
  - Not tried (would need a new protocol): f held fixed, ALIKED dense map, feature layer choice, learned or fine-tuned
    adapters (E7), an edge/skyline channel alongside the features.
