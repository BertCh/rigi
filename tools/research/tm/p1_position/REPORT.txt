P1 - POSITION TRIAGE BEFORE MATCHING (TM program, dev only)            2026-09-28, subagent
=====================================================================================================================
Question: using only metadata (Commons title, stated lat/lon, EXIF altitude, heading) + Mapterhorn DEM + OSM, can we
flag "position suspect" before matching and propose candidate eyes, cheaply?
Rules: PROTOCOL.txt (written 17:48Z before any flag was computed). No rendering, no matching, no live services.
Files: p1.py (flags), fetch_gaz.py + net.py (cached Overpass/Nominatim), evaluate_p1.py (scoring -> eval.txt),
posthoc.py (POST HOC variant -> flags_posthoc.json), flags.json, proposals.json, cache/ (86 cached responses, 79 MB).

HEADLINE
- Titles carry usable position text for a minority of photos: 19/50 have a viewpoint phrase, 16 resolve to an OSM
  feature; 21 name a target peak in the 40 km gazetteer; 29/50 have either. 21/50 give the triage nothing to work with.
- Pre-registered primary flag (F-far | F-low | F-vis): 12 flagged. Against F1's 9 position-stage failures:
  TP 3 / FP 9 / FN 6 (precision 0.25, recall 0.33). Against the 5 a_big (>400 m) cases: TP 3 / FN 2 (recall 0.60).
  COST: 6 of the 12 flags land on photos that already have a verified-correct pose at the stated eye
  (0009, 0017, 0020, 0046, 0059, 0094) - i.e. 6/30 (20%) of the solvable photos would be falsely called suspect.
- The flag that works is F-far (viewpoint phrase geocodes > 400 m away): 6 flagged, catches the two headline cases
  (wc_0069 Fronalpstock 1.08 km, eye 575 m too low - F1 estimated ~600 m; wc_0073 Pilatus Kulm 17.5 km, eye 1621 m too
  low - F1 estimated ~16 km) with only 1 flag on a solved photo (wc_0017 "Bellwald- Aussicht": the named village's
  aerialway station 2.2 km away and 813 m BELOW the verified eye).
- F-vis (target peak not LOS-visible or > 60 deg off heading) is net harmful: 6 flags, 1 TP (wc_0087, by a partial
  name match "du midi" -> Pointe du Midi), 5 on solved photos. Causes: toponyms that are pass/railway names, not
  targets ("Ospizio Bernina", "Berninabahn" -> Piz Bernina hidden), the "Viewpoint Target" title convention
  ("Eggishorn Matterhorn": Eggishorn treated as a target, 153 deg behind the camera), generic names ("First" 25 km),
  and manifest headings that disagree with the named targets (wc_0020: 83 deg).
- F-low never fired (no viewpoint within 1 km sat > 150 m above the eye). F-exif fired once (wc_0028, EXIF alt 570 m
  above the DEM) - on a photo whose verified ref uses exactly that EXIF eye (1808 m), so it is a real inconsistency
  (lat/lon probably off, altitude right) but not a failure.
- POST HOC (designed after seeing the results; n = 2 positives; treat as a hypothesis for the next frozen test):
  "F-far-up" = F-far AND the named viewpoint is >= 150 m ABOVE the stated eye. On flags.json it flags exactly
  {0037, 0069, 0073}: 2 TP, 1 FP (0037, a same-name hamlet 22 km away), 0 flags on solved photos. With the post hoc
  resolver (no place=* as viewpoints, nearest over exact+prefix) it flags {0069, 0073} only. Rationale: people name
  the high vantage ("from Pilatus"); a named village/valley station below the stated eye (0017, 0053) is not evidence
  of a bad pin. Also post hoc: dropping F-vis entirely removes 5 of the 6 costly flags.

1. SETUP
- 50 dev ids (split.json), manifest title/lat/lon/altitudeM/headingDeg/positionSource. Stated eye recomputed as
  max(alt ?? ground, ground + 1.6) (src/lib/geo/pipeline.ts) from Mapterhorn z14; matches the C0 cache meta.eye to
  <= 0.25 m on all 50.
- Gazetteer: OSM within 40 km (peaks/volcanoes/saddles/ridges, viewpoints, alpine/wilderness huts, shelters, place
  nodes, railway stations/halts, aerialway stations), 4.4k-18.8k named features per photo.
- Name matching: accent/umlaut folding, spaces removed variant, one generic leading token (Piz/Monte/Mount/...), exact
  match on the capture's leading 1-5 tokens, then prefix match (>= 5 chars), nearest feature wins; Nominatim fallback
  used once (wc_0067 "Schwendisee" -> Vorderer Schwendisee 223 m).
- LOS: eye -> summit (DEM max in a 30 m disc, +0 m), curvature + refraction k = 0.13, z14/z12/z10 bands as dem.py,
  obstruction tolerance 20 m.

5. DEVIATIONS / IMPLEMENTATION NOTES (honest log)
  - Gazetteer query: the protocol's per-photo query (incl. place=* ways/relations) took 2-10 min each on the public
    Overpass (429/504s). After 21 photos had been fetched with the full query, the remaining 29 used place=* NODES only;
    for uniformity place ways/relations were dropped from all 50 (gazetteer() in fetch_gaz.py). This change was made
    after a debug run on 9 photos (none of them flagged by F-far/F-low) and before the full results; it is a
    data-source change, not a threshold change. A few responses came from a mirror with an older OSM base date
    (2026-05/07).
  - Bug fixed before the full run: the viewpoint span used to extend past the stop word (hiding targets after
    "auf den ...").
  - Known parser weaknesses left as-is (frozen): a "da" cue inside "Lägh da l'Albigna as seen from Piz Blanch" captures
    a junk phrase (unresolved, harmless); "Ausblick" matches the suffix-view rule with X="aus" (unresolved); "Evening
    view from ..." yields a junk "evening" capture (unresolved). Nominatim was queried for unresolved junk (cached).
  - All post hoc numbers are labelled; the primary numbers above are the pre-registered ones.

6. LIMITATIONS
  - n is tiny: 5 a_big positives, 2 of which drive every positive result. F1 labels are unblinded visual judgements
    (+-2-3 photos), and "position" for wc_0035/0087/0095 is an estimate.
  - Title text is Commons-specific. App uploads have no title at all; there the only inputs would be EXIF GPS/alt,
    heading, and whatever the user types.
  - Nearest-match disambiguation is fragile for common names (Santa Maria, First, Rothorn, Bernina) and for
    pass/railway names containing a peak name.
  - LOS at z10 beyond 15 km smooths ridges; +0 m target with 20 m tolerance is a coarse test; targets > 40 km are not in
    the gazetteer (e.g. Matterhorn from Eggishorn), so the check is silent for exactly the long-range photos.
  - Headings in the manifest are partly template values of unknown quality; the 60 deg rule punished them.

7. WHAT A PRODUCTION VERSION (app upload path) WOULD NEED
  - Inputs: EXIF GPS + altitude + direction when present; an optional free-text "taken from" field or the user's
    caption; for Commons-like imports, the title/description. Cache a regional gazetteer offline (Overpass extract or a
    pre-built peak/viewpoint/station/hut table per region) - the public Overpass is far too slow (2-10 min/query) for an
    upload path; Nominatim only as a rare fallback with its usage policy (1 req/s, caching, UA).
  - Use ONLY the up-hill far check (F-far-up, to be re-validated on a frozen split before use): viewpoint phrase ->
    feature >= 400 m away AND >= 150 m above the stated eye -> "position suspect" + candidate eye at the feature
    (summit/top station + 2 m) fed to the existing <= 400 m eye search and the T6 matcher, reporting both results
    rather than silently moving the eye. Do not ship F-vis in this form; if kept, require an explicit target cue
    ("Blick auf", "view of", "vers") and disable the heading clause unless the heading came from EXIF.
  - Disambiguation: prefer peaks/viewpoints/top stations over places; choose among exact+prefix matches jointly;
    reject same-name features > 15-20 km away unless nothing closer exists.
  - An EXIF sanity readout (|GPS alt - DEM| > 150 m) is cheap and worth surfacing (wc_0028: +570 m) but it is an
    inconsistency indicator, not a failure predictor.
  - Expected value on this dev set: at best 2 photos (0069, 0073) move from "hopeless at the stated eye" to "worth a
    T6 run at a proposed eye"; the triage cannot touch the 4 small-eye-error cases, which need the parallax /
    PnP-shift eye refinement from X5/F1 instead.
