X5 — negative-evidence pose verifier (TM program)            2026-09-28, DEV ids only, post-hoc items labelled
==========================================================================================================================

QUESTION
Can negative evidence (structure the render predicts that the photo lacks, or geometric inconsistency across depth)
reject wrong-basin / wrong-eye poses that positive evidence accepts, without rejecting correct poses?

SHORT ANSWER
Partly. No single negative-evidence family is a safe general verifier on this data. One geometric check does work on
the eye-error traps. It was found post hoc, so it needs pre-registered confirmation:
  * PARALLAX / FREE-CENTRE CHECK (pnp_rel, POST HOC): solve 6-DoF PnP on the lifted matches (hypothesis focal) and
    measure how far it wants to move the camera centre, divided by the median depth of its inliers.
    - Every correct ref with >= 100 inliers: pnp_rel <= 0.032.
    - Caught: wc_0001 B 0.049, wc_0070 A 0.068 / C 0.052, wc_0002 A 0.094, wc_0028 T6A 0.18, wc_0006 CPU 0.048.
    - Not caught: wc_0069 A 0.013 and wc_0074 A 0.013 (inside the correct range), and wc_0086 N7 0.031 (borderline).
  * X2's mono-geometry score (EXTERNAL) catches exactly the other pair (wc_0069 A, wc_0074 A) and misses wc_0001 B and
    wc_0070 A. The two checks are complementary.
    - OR-veto, population inl >= 100: 6/7 hard wrongs caught, 2/22 correct refs lost (wc_0047 A, wc_0085 C, both
      correct v2 HIGHs). Missed: wc_0086 N7 (no X2 score).
    - The operating point is fragile. With a population of inl >= 30, one weakly-matched correct ref (wc_0071 C,
      85 inliers, pnp_rel 0.055) raises the LOPO threshold, and wc_0001 B is no longer caught.
  * The pre-specified candidates are weak on the hard wrongs (n = 7): contour coverage, skyline residual, ring hypothesis
    spread, re-match gain and NFA. Hard-wrong AUROC is 0.40–0.69, except the app skyline score (sg_score / sky_edge,
    0.78–0.86).
  * Far-solve -> near/mid band consistency separates perfectly where it can be computed (rot_far_near, res_far_near
    AUROC 1.0). It exists for only 6 correct refs and 1 wrong: fixed km bands rarely hold >= 12 matches in both bands
    for a single ALIKED view. It is a sparse-data result, not a verifier.
  * wc_0070 A and wc_0001 B (the main session's question): caught by pnp_rel, not by X2 or X4. But wc_0070 A has only
    30 single-view ALIKED inliers here, versus 330 fused in T6, so it is a weak case either way.
  * Veto on top of T6/v2: no LR model is usable as a veto at "zero wrong accepted". They veto 3–19 of the 19 correct
    HIGHs. Only the single-feature LOPO vetoes spare the HIGHs: pnp_rel loses 1 correct HIGH (wc_0047), X2 loses 1
    (wc_0085). None of them vetoes wc_0086 N7, except pnp_up_abs (which loses wc_0027) and the X2-containing LR (via
    median imputation, which does not count).

DATA (n)
  Positives: 32 correct refs over 30 photos.
  Negatives: 128 wrong refs over 43 photos, plus wc_0086 N7 (the v2 moved-eye HIGH, verified wrong near-miss).
    - N7 was rendered by render_extra.py under render_lock on port 8791. Eye matches key.json exactly.
    - The same pose at the stated eye (T8) renders into a slope: 0 matches, unlabelled, excluded.
  "Hard" wrongs = wrong refs with >= 100 hypothesis inliers: 7. These are wc_0001 B, 0002 A, 0006 CPU, 0028 T6A,
    0069 A, 0074 A and 0086 N7. wc_0070 A/C have 30/64. All other wrongs have < 30 single-view inliers, so positive
    evidence alone kills them.
  Perturb: 360 views (30 photos × 12 offsets), scored and never used for training.
  Per-photo ranking uses the 23 photos that have both a correct and a wrong ref.
  LEAKAGE WARNING: the hard wrongs come from 7 photos. Every CV below is leave-one-photo-out (LOPO). The key trap
  results are single cases and carry no statistical weight.

METHOD
  Matcher
    - ALIKED (4096 kp, det 0.01) + LightGlue on CPU (core.lg_match), with s1.correspond conventions: photo resized to the
      render size, one view per pose.
    - Lifting through the cached half-res xyz, with match.lift checks: sky, < 250 m, 5×5 range spread (at stride 2 this
      is ±4 native px).
    - Matches: 1,715 pairs (refs, perturb, 12-view ring at 30° steps, extra), stored in raw/.
    - The ring was cut from 24 to 12 views because of CPU load from concurrent studies.
    - LoMa comparison NOT done: the GPU was shared and time was limited.
  Features: all at the hypothesis P (its render, eye and intrinsics). THR = 6 px native.
    - Positive: inliers, fraction, 4×3 coverage, rmse, drift, a-contrario log10 NFA (fixed hypothesis, uniform
      background, Chernoff bound on underflow).
    - (1) Depth bands, near < 1 km / mid 1–5 km / far > 5 km: per-band RANSAC rotation, transfer ratio (the far
      rotation's inlier rate on mid/near relative to their own best), inter-band rotation angle, and median residual.
      Variant q_* splits at the median inlier depth, so it is always available.
    - (1b, POST HOC, added after the first band results) PnP free centre: pnp_shift/up/dist/gain, and
      pnp_rel = |C| / median inlier depth (pnp_rel10 uses the 10th percentile).
    - (2) Occluding contours from xyz: |Δ log depth| > 0.3, near side >= 300 m, skyline band excluded.
      Coverage = the jump-weighted fraction with a multi-scale Canny edge within 2 grid px and orientation within
      about ±34°, minus a chance level from 8 shifted edge maps. Also near/far splits, and the converse
      (edge_explained). TEED NOT used: Canny is permitted, and no weights were downloaded.
    - (3) Skyline: rendered skyline vs the photo P(sky) boundary (app pose-free sky model), giving sky_med and
      sky_frac3. sky_edge = Canny support along the rendered skyline. sg_score = the app's skyline score at P.
    - (4) Ring spread: per-ring-view rotation (+focal) solve.
      - ring_comp_ratio = best other-basin support (> 20° away) vs own-basin support (<= 10°).
      - hyp_vs_comp = hypothesis inliers vs best other basin.
      - SUE dispersion.
    - (5) rematch_gain = hypothesis-render inliers vs nearest-basin ring support (ring at the stated eye).
  Models: L2 logistic regression with <= 4 features and median imputation (both fitted in-fold).
    - Thresholds for "zero wrong accepted" (nested):
      - Each held-out photo is judged against the maximum training-wrong score of its fold.
      - "oracle" = the threshold picked on the pooled out-of-fold scores (optimistic).
    - M4 = forward selection nested inside each LOPO fold (inner: grouped 5-fold).

RESULTS 4 — simulated veto on top of T6/v2 (21 v2 HIGHs: 19 correct, wc_0076 unsure, wc_0086 wrong)
  The final pose of every correct HIGH is within 0.2° of a cached correct ref; that ref's features were used.
  wc_0086 uses N7.
  Single-feature veto (tau = LOPO extreme over other photos' correct refs, population inl >= 100):
    pnp_rel             -> vetoes 1 correct HIGH (wc_0047), not wc_0086
    X2 (ext)            -> vetoes 1 correct HIGH (wc_0085), not wc_0086
    pnp_rel OR X2       -> vetoes 2 correct HIGHs (wc_0047, wc_0085), not wc_0086
    pnp_up_abs          -> vetoes wc_0086, and 1 correct HIGH (wc_0027)
    pnp_up_abs OR X2    -> vetoes wc_0086, and 2 correct HIGHs (wc_0027, wc_0085)
  LR models at the nested zero-wrong threshold veto 3–19 correct HIGHs, so they are unusable as a veto.
  Verdict: every veto that catches something costs at least 1 of 19 correct HIGHs on dev. The T6 frozen rule already
  has 0 wrong HIGH at the stated eye, so a veto only pays if it enables a looser rule, LoMa or eye search. The looser
  rule is where these traps would appear.

INTERPRETATION
  1. Why contours and skyline fail on the traps: the traps are near-miss eyes and similar-looking basins, and their
     rendered structure largely exists in the photo.
     - wc_0086 N7 has the best contour lift of all poses (0.64).
     - wc_0069 A has 0.40 (correct median about 0.2).
     - Canny cannot tell "missing" from "hazy/low-contrast", and correct refs of hazy photos score as low as the traps.
  2. Why fixed depth bands fail: single-view ALIKED matches rarely populate two bands. Median-split bands (q_*) are
     always available, but they are weak (0.69).
  3. What works: asking the geometry directly whether a different centre explains the matches better, with the
     answer normalised by depth.
     - This is the "parallax-based wrong-eye veto" that F1 proposed, done as a free-centre PnP rather than as bands.
     - It sees eye errors (0001, 0070, 0002, 0028) and cannot see wrong basins at the right eye (0069) or eye errors
       masked by far-only inliers (0074: +83 m up, but at a 6.9 km median depth).
     - Mono-depth ordinal geometry (X2) sees the latter and not the former.
  4. The ring hypothesis spread (SUE/basin competitor) is dominated by inlier count, and the 12-view ring is coarse.
     No value beyond log_inl.

CAVEATS
  - Hard wrongs: n = 7 (+2 at 30–64 inliers), 7 photos. Every trap statement is n = 1.
  - pnp_rel was designed after seeing pnp_dist values (post hoc). It needs a pre-registered test on new negatives
    (mined skyline top-k, the LoMa arm's wrong finals) before any use.
  - Single-view ALIKED is weaker than T6's fused multi-view matches: many correct refs have < 100 single-view
    inliers (wc_0002 C 1, 0034/0052/0055 0), so they are unrecoverable by any matching-based verifier here.
  - The X2 score is missing for N7, 1 correct ref (0027 A) and 17 wrong refs. Where X2 enters an LR, missing values are
    median-imputed.
  - Ring cut to 12 views (30°). The ring is at the stated eye only.

RECOMMENDATION
  - Do not deploy a learned veto from this dev data.
  - Pre-register a two-part geometric veto for the next arm: pnp_rel (> ~0.035 at >= 100 inliers) OR X2 combo_int.z
    (< X2's tau). Test it on mined negatives: LoMa and eye-search finals, and skyline top-k minus the correct pose.
    Expected dev cost: 1–2 of 19 correct HIGHs.
  - Compute pnp_rel on the fused multi-view T6/LoMa correspondences, not single views. Also report the PnP
    centre-shift vector: it doubles as an eye-refinement proposal (wc_0074: +83 m up; wc_0086: +79 m up).
