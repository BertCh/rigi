COMMON SUPPORT (every candidate that passes the pre-screen)
- Render ONE sat view at the candidate pose (yaw, pitch, roll, vfov) at the candidate's own eye (lat, lon, h;
  adhoc alt = round(h, 2) unless within 0.05 m of the stated-eye page's eye), render size = the worker's (1024 long
  side, photo aspect). LoMa-B (4096 kp, MPS fp32, filter 0.1) photo <-> that view via correspond_loma (service lift).
- common_inl = number of lifted matches whose reprojection residual under the candidate pose at the achieved eye
  is < 6.0 px (fusion.SUPPORT_PX = the service solve threshold). Also stored: lifted count, a rotation solve
  (s1.solve, prior = candidate pose) -> solve inliers, solved pose, drift (deg). If the achieved eye differs from
  the candidate eye by > 0.5 m it is flagged.
- Pre-screen (compute bound): common support is computed for every candidate with a pose EXCEPT
  (i) single-view solves / stage-1-only / probe hypotheses with native inliers < 30, (ii) T6-family verified
  candidates matched with LoMa whose fused inliers < 30, (iii) sky-only unverified hypotheses (no support number).
  Skipped candidates stay in pool.json with status "not-screened".

POOL RULE (as given by the main session, verbatim intent)
- Keep candidates with common support >= 100 OR native T6 fused support >= 100.
- Merge within a photo: yaw & pitch within 0.5 deg AND eye within 2 m 3-D -> one candidate (keep all provenance).
  Merge happens before common support; the representative pose is the member from the highest-priority source
  (S1 > S5 > S2 > S3 > S4 > S0), ties by native support.
- Inherit: a candidate within 0.5 deg yaw/pitch and 2 m eye of an existing blind verdict (refs.correct_refs /
  wrong_refs at the manifest lat/lon + eyeH, and tools/matcher/v2/verify verdicts.json + the round-1 stated-eye T8
  verdict) inherits it and is not re-verified. Only "correct"/"wrong" verdicts are inherited; "unsure" is not.
- Wrong by construction (amendment 1): a non-inherited candidate whose eye is within 2 m of the eye of a verified-
  CORRECT ref of the same photo and whose yaw or pitch differs by > 3 deg from EVERY such correct ref is
  label = "wrong-construct". It does not count toward the cap. A seeded random 20% of them (at least 5) go into the
  blind pack as a label-noise check.
- Cap per photo: at most 6 new (non-inherited, non-construct) candidates, chosen by common support (ties: native)
  after a 3 deg yaw NMS among candidates whose eyes are within 2 m of each other. S4 pilot candidates counted
  separately: max 3 per photo. Candidates beyond the cap stay in pool.json with status "capped".
- pool.json per candidate: pid, cid, sources (all provenance), pose, eye{lat,lon,h}, eyeDisplacementM vs stated,
  common_inl (+ solve fields), native support fields, inherit verdict or null, label (null | wrong-construct |
  pilot), status (blind | inherited | wrong-construct | capped | below-threshold | not-screened).
