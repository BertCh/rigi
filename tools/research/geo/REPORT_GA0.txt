5. DESIGN DECISIONS (fixed before data)
  Floor radius: clamp(hAcc,5,100) + 30 m instead of the plan's 500 m + hAcc, plus a 3 m drop guard for
    shore-strip eligibility. Reservoirs and basins are skipped, and so is flowing water. Why: land below a lake
    within 500 m is real (dam feet, polders, outlet valleys), and there a floor would lift a correct eye.
  Declination is applied where the heading is used (engine constructors, unknown-pose), never where it is
    stored, so IndexedDB keeps the EXIF value.
  SWISS_LAKE_LEVELS were compiled from reference knowledge and NOT re-downloaded (+-0.5 m). They are used only
    when OSM has no ele.

6. MATCHER PATCH (proposal only; tools/matcher is f0's): out/geocam/ga0/matcher-correspondences.patch
  app.py: assemble() becomes a thin wrapper over assemble_core(). With returnCorrespondences the result carries
  correspondences = {W, H, eye, uv, X, nInliers, supportPx}: the inliers within SUPPORT_PX (6 px) at the
  RETURNED pose, round-robin thinned on a 16x12 grid to at most 400, rounded to 0.01 px / 0.01 m. It is wired
  for photoId, multipart and ad-hoc (v034 and t6). Off means byte-identical responses.
  `git apply --check` passes. The helper was unit-tested on synthetic data (1788 inliers -> 400).
  Caveat (plan risk 2): match.py drops < 250 m and depth-edge pixels, so these points mainly constrain rotation.
