A NOTE ON THE DIFFERENTIAL RESIDUAL (analysis, not data): a single junction's two contour offsets carry NO eye
information once a free local image translation (rotation) is allowed - any (e_near, e_far) pair is a translation
of J. r = e_far - e_near/(n_near.n_far) cancels only translation along n_far (pitch-like); translation along the far
tangent leaks with gain tan(angle). Eye information from junctions therefore needs rotation pinned elsewhere (far
skyline, other junctions): the report's "rotation cancels" holds only for near-parallel depth layers.

WHY IT FAILS (post-hoc diagnosis, labelled)
  1. The true eye does not look best on real edges: cost(truth) = 13-38 px^2 (only 2/3 .. 4/6 junctions have both
     contours matched within 12 px at the reference pose), while wrong eyes that predict exactly 3 junctions find
     clutter edges within 12 px for all of them (cost < 1 px^2, e.g. wc_0020 at 70 m). Real photos are full of
     forest, snow, rock-band, building and shadow edges that the DEM lacks; the near contours at 1-3 km are also
     where the DEM rim slides and forests sit on the silhouette.
  2. The cost is a mean over the junctions predicted AT the candidate eye, so eyes with fewer (3) junctions have
     fewer chances to miss. A fixed-identity junction set would need tracking across eyes (the factor does this
     locally via matchJunction, which is only valid within ~40 px).
  3. The information is local: the synthetic well is ~10-20 m wide, so from 25-100 m away only a global search can
     find it, and a global search on real edges is dominated by (1).
  4. Coverage is low (6/40 eligible) for the kill criterion's ">= 3 crossings within 3 km" condition.

HONEST CAVEATS
  - Reference eyes (GT, C0 refs) are not surveyed; for 7059 the reference eye is 12 m below the DEM.
  - n = 6 photos (1 GT); CIs are Wilson intervals over cases that are correlated within a photo.
  - The search is a global lattice search in the window (a local solver could only do worse). Focal is fixed.
  - The junctionFactor (MAP use) was only validated synthetically; it was not scored on real data because the
    scorer-level test already fails.
  - Amendments A (foreground cut 100 m, smoothed eye height, junction completeness/merge) and B (5 m lattice) were
    made after seeing synthetic (render-only) surfaces, before any real-photo number; v1 results are kept.
WHAT MIGHT RESCUE IT (not tested; for the record)

  junction TOPOLOGY (ordering of which ridge occludes which along the skyline) instead of pixel offsets; an
  a-contrario significance on the junction match rather than a truncated mean.
