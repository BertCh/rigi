GEO GA1 PRE-STUDY - PnP WITH PRIOR / COVARIANCE ("AFTERNOON TEST")                          2026-09-30 (Agent C)
Rule: PROTOCOL.txt section PNP (written 03:10Z, before any real-data number). Code: pnp_prior.py.
Data: out/geocam/python/pnp_prior.json (summary + one record per hypothesis x method). Dev ids only; no renders.
Status: a descriptive pre-study for GA1, NOT the GA1 kill test. The GA1-style numbers are indicative.

ENVIRONMENT
  pycolmap 4.2.1, gtsam 4.3.0, pygeomag 1.1.0: cp312 macOS arm64 wheels exist. They are installed with
  `pip install --no-deps --target tools/research/geo/.pylib` into the matcher venv's Python 3.12.13 (212 MB), so no
  3.11 venv was needed. numpy 2.5.3, scipy and poselib 2.0.5 come from tools/matcher/.venv.
  Gotchas:
  (1) torch and pycolmap each ship a libomp, and importing both aborts the process (OMP #15). pnp_prior therefore
      execs the solver functions of tools/matcher/match.py verbatim, via ast, without importing match.py.
  (2) pycolmap 4.2.1 DOES support a position prior: AbsolutePoseRefinementOptions.use_position_prior /
      position_prior_in_world / position_prior_covariance. This resolves plan risk 1.
  (3) Its default gradient_tolerance = 1.0 stops the refinement early. With that default a 20 m prior never moved
      the centre, so 1e-10 is used.
  (4) return_covariance gives a 6x6 over [rotation(3), t(3)] of cam_from_world, for UNIT pixel noise (not scaled by
      the residual). It was checked against Monte-Carlo and against our own J^T J to 1e-4.

WHAT WAS RUN
  Hypotheses (skipped if < 12 correspondences; 44 skipped, listed in the json):
  - E1 primary: 32 POS (REF plus H1 POOL correspondences at the stated eye) and 46 NE-dec (150/400 m displaced
    eyes).
  - E1 NE-inh: 5 (no eye truth).
  - E1 dispfull extras: 10 NE-dec and 13 AMB (50 m).
  - E3: 26 T (true eye, near field kept), plus 25 G20, 22 G50 and 16 G150.
  Methods, all with a 6 px RANSAC threshold and fixed f0 unless stated:
    ROT   rotation only at the hypothesis eye
    PNP6  PoseLib 6-DoF
    P4PF  PoseLib P4Pf, free focal
    COL0  pycolmap, no prior
    COLP  pycolmap with a 20 m position prior
    FUSE  COL0 fused with the prior by information addition
    UP2P  PoseLib upright 2-point with gravity taken from the hypothesis pose, then 4-DoF LM
  Sigmas are Laplace (J^T J)^-1 s^2 with s = 1.4826 MAD of the inlier residual components (COLP adds the prior).
  pycolmap's own covariance x s^2 is also stored.

PRE-DECLARED QUESTIONS
  1. Covariance calibration (GA1 wants median (err/sigma)^2 in [0.5, 2]; over-confident by > 3x means > 9):
     COL0 median z^2 per axis (E, N, U):
       POS  5200 / 7400 / 385  -> sigma too small by 72x / 86x / 20x
       E3 T  486 /  686 / 188  -> 22x / 26x / 14x
     COLP is 53x / 75x / 16x on POS.
     => A point-only Laplace covariance is over-confident by 1-2 orders of magnitude. That is far past the GA1 3x
     kill line if used as is.
  2. Pitch, COLP vs ROT on POS (GA1 wants "not worse by > 0.02 deg"): paired medians 0.239 vs 0.182 deg
     (+0.055 deg), and on E3 T 0.090 vs 0.057 (+0.033). Freeing the centre makes pitch WORSE, the same failure as
     the old P4Pf.
  3. FLAG rule (COL0 Mahalanobis d^2 of the hypothesis eye > 11.34):
       NE-dec  46/46 flagged, CI [0.92, 1.00]
       POS     32/32 flagged (false alarms), CI [0.89, 1.00]
       E3 T    24/25; E3 G50 20/20; E3 G20 22/23; E3 G150 14/14
       AMB (50 m) 13/13; NE-inh 5/5
     => The rule flags EVERYTHING. The descriptive bar (>= 50% detection at <= 10% false alarm) fails. The raw
     d^2 does not separate POS from NE-dec at all (AUROC 0.49).
  Answer to "does the covariance/centre estimate flag the wrong eye?": NO. With appearance matches (> 250 m in E1;
  near kept but rarely matched in E3) the PnP centre is not an eye measurement. It drifts 30-300 m from a CORRECT
  eye, while claiming 1-6 m.

POST HOC (seen after scoring; hypotheses for later, not findings)
  - The size of the centre shift carries SOME signal. AUROC for NE-dec > POS:
      P4PF shift 0.87 (POS median 37 m vs NE-dec 332 m)
      COL0 0.67, PNP6 0.67, UP2P 0.65, COLP 0.59
    E3 (G50 + G150 vs T): 0.67-0.77.
    P4PF's free focal seems to soak up the systematic error that otherwise drives the centre, so its centre drifts
    least on correct eyes. n is small, and this was not pre-registered.
  - The inflation needed to make COL0 calibrated (about 20-100x in sigma) is much larger than the grid-thinning
    correction the plan proposes (nEff 60 of ~1000 matches is about 4x). The residuals are systematic: DEM/render vs
    photo, lens distortion, LoMa bias. They are not iid, so a MAD rescale alone cannot fix it.
  - Clean subset: hypotheses where ROT gets >= 100 inliers AND is within 0.3 deg of the verified pose. Only 5 E1 POS
    photos and 12 E3 T photos qualify. Many POS correspondence sets are mostly outliers; for example wc_0002 refC has
    a 104 px median residual at its own pose.
    On this subset, free-centre drift is smaller but still real:
      E1 POS: COL0 eTrue 33 m, sigma_EN 0.7 m, sigma still too small by 13-20x.
      E3 T:   COL0 eTrue 26 m, sigma still too small by 6-15x.
    Pitch: ROT 0.017 deg vs COLP 0.073 deg on E1 POS, and 0.028 vs 0.050 deg on E3 T. The conclusion holds on clean
    data.
  - UP2P pitch 0.02 deg on POS is a gravity-oracle artefact, since gravity comes from a hypothesis pose that is
    within 0.5 deg of truth. It says nothing about a real IMU.

IMPLICATIONS FOR GA1 (Agent B)
  - Point factors should constrain ROTATION. Do not let them move the eye (guard-rail 1). This agrees with E0, E1
    and E3.
  - If point factors enter the MAP with the eye free, their information must be deflated heavily, or the eye block
    must be fixed or marginalised with a strong prior. Otherwise the solve reports sigma_EN of ~2 m on photos where
    the eye is really unobserved, and GA1's "all-far photos report sigma_EN >= 0.8 sigma_GPS" fails by
    construction.
  - Suggested (untested) point-factor sigma: hypot(sigma_px, f * sigma_DEM(d) / d) PLUS a per-photo systematic
    term. Alternatively, calibrate nEff so that the point family's eye information matches this study's observed
    eTrue spread.

CAVEATS
  - The "true eye" for POS and E3 T is the stated manual/EXIF eye, not surveyed. Part of a 30-270 m drift could, in
    principle, be the truth being wrong. The drifts are too large and too common for that to be the main story, and
    E3's 3/30 result agrees.
  - E1 correspondences are LoMa, lifted with the 250 m cut; E3's are ALIKED with near points kept. Both are from a
    single view.
  - E3 UP2P and the E3 hypothesis rotation use the ref pose, so E3 gravity is truth.
