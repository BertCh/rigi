WHAT THE REFERENCE IS
  A GTSAM 4.3.0 graph over the same parameters as core/types.ts:
    R = (yaw, pitch, roll) in degrees, as a Vector3
    F = logf, as a Vector1
    E = (E, N, U) in metres, as a Vector3
  Fixed blocks are held as constants.
  Every TS factor row becomes one gtsam.CustomFactor. Its robust loss is a noiseModel.mEstimator.Custom with weight
  a*psi(z) and loss a*rho(z)/2, where psi and rho follow map/lm.ts and a is the TS grid-thinning factor.
  Factors mirrored (Python ports, not calls into TS):
    - gps, alt
    - ground (asymmetric sigma)
    - gravity
    - compass, Student-t nu = 3 (via the custom mEstimator)
    - focal
    - point 2D-3D, sigma = sigma_px (+) f sqrt(1-rho) sigma_DEM(d)/d
    - skyline, with the eye-linearised horizon, columnSigma per sample, and the horizons recomputed in Python from
      the fixture ridge
    - the map/cluster.ts correlated-DEM whitening (I + s_c^2 G G^T)^-1/2, for both point and skyline
  The camera model is a Python port of camera/index.ts and concord camera-x.ts (fScale, k1, principal point).
  Optimiser: gtsam.LevenbergMarquardtOptimizer.
  Covariance: gtsam.Marginals joint marginal of the free keys. It uses a second graph whose data rows carry 1/s^2,
  with s = max(1, MAD scale) as in map/covariance.ts.
  Jacobians: central differences with small steps (1e-5 deg, 1e-7, 1e-3 m).
  Parity mirrors TS's pass structure exactly:
    - the MAP of TS's last inner solve (the linearisation of relinAll pass k-1, thinning at that state), solved
      from the fixture's x0;
    - the covariance at the TS solution with the final pass's linearisation;
    - the skyline no-op rule when the quantised eye is unchanged is replayed.

OBSERVATIONS (not parity items)
  - syn_far_all: sigma_E/N = 17.4/16.9 m against a 20 m GPS prior, i.e. 0.84-0.87 sigma_GPS. That is the GA1
    "all-far photos report sigma_EN >= 0.8 sigma_GPS" behaviour, and it comes from the cluster whitening.
  - real_wc_0067_k000: sigma_E/N = 9.9/16.9 m. The same photo's plain point-only Laplace (REPORT_PNP) claims about
    1 m. The cluster model removes most of the eye over-confidence found in the PnP pre-study, but whether the
    sigmas are calibrated can only be judged on data (GA1 eval).
  - syn_skyline_eye: a 2 km ridge skyline alone leaves sigma_EN at ~18 m (the cluster part absorbs most of it).

CAVEATS
  - B's map code was still changing during this session (point/skyline clusters appeared, and the solveMap trust
    term was replaced by an eye step cap). The results above are for the code as of 2026-09-30T03:40Z. Re-run:
      npx tsx tools/research/geo/ts_fixture_solve.ts out/geocam/python/fixtures/*.json &&
      PYTHONPATH=tools/research/geo/.pylib tools/matcher/.venv/bin/python tools/research/geo/gtsam_ref.py parity
    Any later model change in factors.ts or cluster.ts must be mirrored in gtsam_ref.py (build_blocks, point_block,
    sky_state).
  - The concord cue factors (edge / level / shore via cueResidualPx) and the lakes, junction and integrity factors
    are NOT mirrored.
  - Parity uses a FIXED linearisation per pass. The outer relinearisation loop itself (the eye step cap, relinM)
    is only replayed from TS's recorded pass states, not re-derived independently.
  - Fixture ground is a plane. A DEM ground is not in the format.
