WHAT WAS BUILT
  src/lib/geocam/map/{lm,covariance,cluster,factors,solve,index,map.check}.ts
    solveMap(p, x0, o?) -> MapResult (core/types.ts). Dense LM on the free params, per-factor IRLS (l2/huber/cauchy/
    student), nEff thinning, NaN rows dropped (constant penalty in the objective), FD central Jacobians (core steps),
    yaw wrap. Outer loop re-linearises eye-dependent factors; eye steps > trustM (25 m) are clipped (NO proximal prior:
    joint.ts's proximal term shrank the Monte Carlo spread by ~30 % when still active at the stop). Covariance
    (J^T W J)^-1 at the solution, data block / max(1, MAD)^2, perFamily info, sigma_EN = sqrt(lambda_max EN).
    Factors: gps, alt, ground (asymmetric), gravity, compass (Student-t nu 3), focal, skyline (eye-linearised horizon,
    columnSigma per sample, Cauchy 2, nEff 60), point (2D-3D), concordCueFactors (joint.ts cueResidualPx).
    NEW (needed for the all-far criterion): cluster-correlated DEM error (map/cluster.ts). A DEM offset shared by a
    patch of terrain is exactly an eye offset for those rows, so independent-row DEM sigma let 100+ far samples
    "observe" the eye to a few metres. Each factor splits the DEM variance: (1-rho) independent, rho (0.5) as a 3D
    offset per (15 deg sector x distance band) cluster, marginalised in closed form (Woodbury whitening with the rows'
    eye Jacobian). Default ON for skyline and point; cluster: null turns it off.
  scripts/geocam/lib.ts (shared eval helpers, API in its header), ga1-eval.ts, ga1-fixtures.ts.
