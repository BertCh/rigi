  7018 / 7033: ~157 LEVEL cues at 2-8 km (Thunersee from ~220 m above the lake). At the GT camera the level residual
    is a flat -4.4 px in both distance bands (2-4 km: -4.41, > 4 km: -3.84..-4.42; POST HOC diagnostic) - exactly
    concord's cue bias, with no 1/d signature. Dip sensitivity is f/d = 1216/3000 = 0.4 px/m, so -4.4 px equals

HONESTY / CAVEATS
  - n = 4 photos (three on the same lake, Thunersee). The CI is the statement; 16.9 m is not a precise number, but
    even the lower CI bound (6.3 m) fails the bar.
  - GT eyes are not independent (DEM + 1.6 rule; 7053 below the DEM). The error vs GT includes the GT's own error;
    the non-convergence and the dz=0 drift are independent of that.
  - Lake levels: Thunersee from OSM ele 558.0 (+-0.5 m) - not the limiting factor at these error sizes.
  - searchPx 30 (frozen) widens the search enough to cover a 20 m error at 1 km but also admits wrong edges; the
    6971/7053 blow-ups are consistent with wrong-edge lock-in after an outer re-extraction. No other setting was
    tried (no tuning after scoring).
  - Runtime: ~1.5-4 min per solve (7 horizon marches + one 800 px ray cast + cue search per outer iteration).

IMPLICATION: the dip geometry is sound (synthetic check: 0.3 m with a -3 px bias when the distances span 1.5-4.5 km),
  but on the dev lakes the waterline cue is (a) biased by a constant ~ -4 px that is collinear with eye height over a
  narrow distance range, and (b) far away from high viewpoints, where sigma_h ~ d * sigma_theta is > 5 m anyway.
  GA4 as specified does not deliver a 5 m eye height. The lake FLOOR (a veto, GA0) is unaffected by this result.
  A redesign would need a bias-free waterline observation (e.g. the shore/reflection pair giving the axis, which
  concord found worse) or near lakes (< 1.5 km) with level cues, which none of the dev lake photos has.
