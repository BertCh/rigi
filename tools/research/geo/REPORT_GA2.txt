WHAT WAS BUILT
  crlb(p, x, {steps, relinearize, thinning}) -> FisherReport {info, cov, sigma (per DoF), sigmaEye (sqrt lambda_max
      of the E/N block), sigmaU, sigmaEye3, byFamily [{family, n, info, sigmaEyeAlone, share}], cond, mask}.
      Central-difference Jacobians of the whitened residuals (= prediction Jacobian / row sigma), nEff thinning, no
      robust weights, no MAD: the Gaussian CRLB of the model at x. share = family's part of tr(Sigma_EN) from
      Sigma = sum_f Sigma I_f Sigma (shares sum to 1); sigmaEyeAlone = family + all priors; cond in correlation form.
  heldOutFamily(p, full, family, {x0}) -> {before, after, improved, xWithout, eyeMoveM, agreeM}: re-solve without the
      family from the pre-move state; the family's mean robust cost at x0 vs at the held-out solution.
  eyeMayMove(fr, held[], {maxSigmaEyeM: 15, integrity}) -> {ok, reasons}: sigma_eye < 15 m AND a held-out family
      confirms (and its fit agrees with the full fit) AND GA5 passes; fails closed (GA5 missing => shut).
  observe.check.ts: 7/7 synthetic checks pass (CRLB = Monte-Carlo spread of solveMap within 20 %: 0.18 vs 0.16-0.18 m;
      far-only sigma_eye 27.9 m >= 0.8 sigma_GPS with GPS share 0.83; near sigma_eye 0.19 m with point share 1.0;
      free focal + far points ill-conditioned; held-out family confirms a true move and refuses a family agreeing
      with a wrong fix; gate opens only with all three legs).

CONSEQUENCES
  - sigma_eye (CRLB or Laplace) is necessary but NOT sufficient: a tight sigma is often a wrong eye. The gate must
    keep all three legs of guard-rail 4.1 (sigma + held-out family + GA5); eyeMayMove fails closed by design.
  - Keep reporting sigma per DoF (rotation sigmas are sensible: yaw 0.05-0.3 deg, pitch 0.03-0.2 deg except 7155),
    but do not present sigma_eye to users as "position +-N m" until a held-out family confirms it.
  - The Laplace sigma (0.60) ranks slightly better than the CRLB (0.50): the MAD rescale carries misfit
    information that the CRLB by construction ignores (post-hoc observation, not in the rule).
HONEST CAVEATS
  - n = 89 hypotheses from 33 photos; bootstrap by photo. Infinite sigma (hypotheses with 0-1 matches, singular
    rotation) is ranked last (fixed as an implementation bug before the final run: the first run silently dropped
    them, rho 0.41; the rule "over all primary hypotheses" requires them).
  - The E1 truth eye is the stated (manual/EXIF) eye, not surveyed.
