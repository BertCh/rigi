HONESTY / CAVEATS
  - n is small: 33 positives in 31 photos, 56 NE-dec in 29 photos (<= 2 per photo, correlated). CP intervals are per
    hypothesis, not per photo; by-photo intervals would be wider.
  - The alert limits (1.0 / 0.5 deg) were set a priori as product tolerances, not tuned; the sweep shows the verdict
    does not hinge on them at the pre-registered loss definition.
  - The E1 correspondences are single-view LoMa sets thinned to <= 400; the product would have skyline + concord cues
    as further families ("-skyline / -point" subsets) that this dataset does not carry. With skyline as an
    independent family the rotation separation may be better conditioned; untested here.
  - Viewshed has no DSM here (swisstopo COG not fetched); the E1 decoys cannot exercise it (see above).
  - The stricter "binding reading" (0/33 POS) was fixed before scoring. The kill does not depend on it: even the
    oracle cannot get below 1/33.

IMPLICATION: solution separation on appearance correspondences with the eye fixed is a good wrong-BASIN detector
  (NB-con AUROC 0.94, every non-trivial wrong-basin decoy rejected) but not a wrong-EYE detector at an acceptable true
  loss: the displaced eyes that fool E1 are also the ones whose subsets agree. Same conclusion as E1/E3 from another
  angle: appearance matches carry rotation, not eye; eye integrity needs eye-observing families (T-junctions GA3,
  lakes GA4) as separate subsets.
