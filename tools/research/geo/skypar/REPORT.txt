WHY IT FAILS (descriptive; POST HOC labels below never change the verdict)
  - chi2_eye is not calibrated: every one of the 14 non-abstained POS has chi2_eye > 16.27 (median 2404, range
    120 - 75542); the 50 m rule does all the rejecting (6/14 POS have |delta-hat| > 50 m). Skyline detector errors
    are spatially correlated and biased (trees, snow, haze), so the per-column Huber covariance is overconfident by
    orders of magnitude. POST HOC: |delta-hat| of the true eye is median 44 m on POS vs 55 m on NE-dec (the truth is
    "displaced" by detector bias about as far as the 150 m decoys are by the model), i.e. fitted eye shifts of a few
    tens of metres are within what the photo skyline cannot resolve.
  - The model is first order in delta/d. A 150 / 400 m eye error against a ridge at 2 - 4 km is 40 - 200 mrad, tens to
    hundreds of px, beyond the linear regime (synthetic spec: recovery is good at 8 m, loose at 45 m, and
    degrades beyond). A Gauss-Newton loop with a re-marched horizon per step would be needed; not in the protocol.
  - Depth diversity is rare: 58% of the correct poses have a far-only skyline at the fixed threshold.
  - Descriptive only: median |residual| after the fit is 1.75 px on POS vs 8.98 px on NE-dec (non-abstained), so
    the skyline FIT residual itself separates a little; that is the old skyline-score eye-locator result
    (negative-results: "True eye ranks 43rd of 113") in another form.
