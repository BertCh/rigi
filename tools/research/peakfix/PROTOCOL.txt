PEAKFIX (PK): multi-distance peak resection for the eye position             written 2026-09-30, BEFORE any PK number
==================================================================================================================
Question (user, 2026-09-30): can peaks identified exactly, at several layers of distance, position the camera more
exactly? A peak at distance d moves sideways by about δ/d radians when the eye moves δ metres, while a far peak barely
moves. A near/far pair therefore separates eye translation from rotation. That is resection on point landmarks.

Prior art in this repo:
  - GA3 (T-junctions) was killed because interior photo edges are not measurable.
  - E3: near-field matcher points barely exist.
  - REPORT_PNP: free-centre PnP on matcher points drifts by hundreds of metres.
PK differs because its photo measurement is the SKYLINE ONLY. Skyline peaks are local maxima of the sky boundary, the
one contour that is measured reliably, and a skyline often mixes near and far ridges. Interior layers are studied only
as an ORACLE (PK0), to price a future interior detector.

Data: the 10 DEV GT photos (scripts/geocam/lib.ts GT_DEV; tools/concord/pins/PROTOCOL.txt split). Holdout photos,
data_v3 and the wild test set are NOT touched. DEM: Mapterhorn (z17 ≤ 300 m, then the MAPTERHORN levels). Eye truth
= the GT eye (the GPS fix plus the GT eye-height rule) = scene origin [0,0,0]. This is NOT independent truth: iPhone
hAcc is typically 5–35 m, which floors any real-data error. hAcc is reported per photo.

Eye model: horizontal (E, N) free. Height = DEM ground(E, N) + (GT eye height above ground at the origin).
Rotation (yaw, pitch, roll) and focal scale are free per eye (focal within ±8 %). No k1 or principal point.

Arms (identical inner rotation solver; only the eye cost differs):
  DENSE  vertical skyline residual at every photo skyline sample (the existing information source).
  PEAK   2D residuals of skyline peaks: photo local maxima vs DEM apparent-skyline local maxima, which are recomputed at
         every candidate eye, so the shift of the silhouette point is modelled. Symmetric nearest-neighbour
         association with hysteresis thresholds; truncated quadratic loss.
  BOTH   DENSE + PEAK, each normalised to its own mean.
Loss: truncated quadratic (σ 1.5 px, truncation 6σ). Pixels on the 1600 px long-side basis.

Search: global grid E, N ∈ [−400, 400] m at 20 m. Then a 5 m grid ±25 m around each arm's grid minimum. The arm's
estimate is the refined minimum. Rotation at each eye: coarse yaw/pitch scan with the DENSE cost from the app start
pose, then Gauss-Newton per arm.

PK0 observability (GT pose, no photo, no search). Fisher information of (rotation, focal, E, N) by central differences
(±5 m eye, re-marched), σ 1.5 px, from:
  (a) DENSE skyline;
  (b) skyline peaks;
  (c) ORACLE layered peaks: crest profiles in distance layers <1, 1–3, 3–10, 10–30, >30 km. Each layer's top crest per
      azimuth forms a profile; the peaks of every layer profile are counted.
Report CRLB σ_E, σ_N and the horizontal σ for each source, plus the distances of the peaks.

PK1 synthetic (sanity gate; tests the solver, not the world). The "photo" skyline is the GT-eye DEM horizon projected
with the GT camera: samples every 2 px, Gaussian noise σ 1 px vertical. The start rotation is the GT rotation
perturbed by +0.6° yaw, −0.3° pitch, +0.3° roll and +2 % focal. The same grid search is used.
  PK1 FAIL if the PEAK arm's median horizontal error > 15 m over the 10 photos. The method is then dead, whatever PK2
  shows.

PK2 real dev photos. Photo skyline = skySamples(photo) (detectSkyline at 1600 px, every 2nd column, weight ≥ 0.3).
Start rotation = the app solve. Per photo: error = |estimated (E, N) − GT eye| for each arm.
  A photo is NO-CALL for an arm if fewer than 3 photo peaks are matched at its estimate (PEAK and BOTH only). A
  no-call counts as a failure in the recall line and is excluded from the precision line.
  PK2 SURVIVES (worth building on) only if ALL of:
    1. PEAK or BOTH: median horizontal error ≤ 40 m over the 10 photos (no-calls counted as 400 m);
    2. that arm is within 25 m on ≥ 5/10 photos;
    3. that arm's median error ≤ 0.75 × the DENSE median.
  Otherwise KILLED as registered. Report everything, including the arm that failed.
  Secondary (descriptive, no kill): error vs the nearest matched peak's distance; the cost-landscape basin (the second
  minimum ≥ 60 m from the best, and its cost ratio).

Fixed before scoring: σ, truncation, grid, peak prominence (photo ≥ 6 px; model ≥ 6 px at the start focal, with
hysteresis 0.6×), the association gate of 25 px, and the arms. Any change after a PK2 number is a labelled amendment,
and the frozen run is kept.

Code: src/lib/peakfix/** (pure). scripts/peakfix/** (evals). Output: out/peakfix/. Report: tools/research/peakfix/REPORT.txt.

AMENDMENT A1 (2026-09-30, written BEFORE any PK1/PK2 number existed; the aborted first launch wrote no result files)
  Finding (smoke test + PK0 debug on IMG_7053, GT pose): with the march starting at 20 m (geo/horizon.ts), moving the
  eye 5 m puts the photographer's immediate ground (z17 DEM lip at the Niederhorn edge) in front of the camera at
  +3.6° and hides the whole far skyline. The eye cost is then dominated by the unmodellable near field: the exact
  standing spot, railings, eye height and the 1 m DEM within a few tens of metres.
  Change: every horizon / layered march ignores terrain nearer than 150 m (dMin = 150 m) in PK0, PK1 and PK2. Same
  idea as roll/mosaic/ridgelines.ts occludeFrom (250 m there). Peaks nearer than 150 m are out of scope.
  Nothing else changes.

AMENDMENT A2 (2026-09-30, POST HOC: written AFTER the frozen PK1 and PK2 numbers were seen; it cannot rescue the
registered verdict)
  Finding: in PK1, the PEAK arm's inner rotation fit fails AT THE TRUE EYE (IMG_7018: 7/27 items matched, cost 20.9
  vs 7.0 at ±40 m). The yaw/pitch scan leaves the +2 % focal and +0.3° roll start error. Edge peaks are 10–16 px
  off, beyond the 9 px truncation, so IRLS never pulls them in. This is a solver-initialisation defect, not geometry.
  Change (diagnostic run tag _a2 only): PEAK and BOTH start from the DENSE arm's fitted rotation at the same eye
  (scan → DENSE GN → arm GN). DENSE is unchanged. Everything else is identical. The registered verdict is the frozen
  run; _a2 is reported as a labelled post-hoc diagnostic.
