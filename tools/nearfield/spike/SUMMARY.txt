STEP INSIDE P0 SPIKE: DEM anchoring + depth split (offline, numerical)          2026-09-28
==========================================================================================
Question: does DEM anchoring + depth split work?
Short answer: PARTLY.
  - Anchoring is necessary: MoGe-2's own metric scale is wrong by 0.3x to 30x. It is feasible on 27/30 photos.
    Its precision is about 20% (scale-only) or about 11% (affine) median log error, so the design gate
    (< 10% at < 500 m) FAILS.
  - The depth split with DEFAULT_SPLIT produces large false "Object" areas (whole distant ranges) on 10/16 clean
    photos. With re-tuned parameters and a mode-based scale it cleanly isolates trees, plants, vehicles, a train,
    a pylon and branches. It does NOT separate huts or villages beyond about 150 m, and there are no people in this set.
  - The pose-verifier signal is weak: AUC 0.73 against wrong refs and 0.55 against yaw +-2 deg. It works as a
    within-photo tie-breaker at best, never as a gate.

DATA / METHOD
  - DEV ids only. Used the 30 of 50 dev photos that have a correct ref (tools/matcher/v2/refs.py labels via
    cache meta.json). That gives 32 correct-ref views, 55 wrong-ref views, and 6 perturbed views per photo
    (yaw+-2, yaw+-8, pitch+-2).
  - DEM range = |xyz - eye| from the cache view xyz.npz (stride-2 grid, 512 px wide).
  - Model depth: reused x2_geom/geom/<pid>.{moge_l,moge_b,da3_b}.npz (768 wide). No new inference and no GPU use.
    Resampled by nearest neighbour to the xyz grid (same normalised image coordinates; ref views have the photo's aspect).
    z-depth was converted to ray length with the solved intrinsics (variant: MoGe's own predicted fov, which changes little).
    Sky = the MoGe mask. DA3 has no mask, so it borrows MoGe-L's.
  - Fit candidates: model-valid, DEM hit, DEM range in [15 m, R]. The 15 m floor was added after inspection: DEM
    range to the ground near the eye is dominated by eye-height and DEM-resolution error (grazing rays). In
    wc_0006 and wc_0099 the DEM puts the foreground at 2-8 m where the model says 10-80 m.
    Fits: scale-only IRLS (Tukey on log ratio, +-25 % inlier band), affine D = s*d + t, power law
    D = e^a d^b, and the mode of the log(D/d) histogram.
  - Split proxy labels: I grouped photos from the manifest foreground tags. CLEAN (16) = no discrete near objects
    expected, so Object area there is ~ false positives. OBJ (7) = trees, plants, cars, train, pylon or branches
    expected (wc_0046, 0054, 0055, 0059, 0067, 0072, 0076). These are coarse proxies, not hand-labelled masks.

1. MoGe metric scale is NOT ~1 (moge_l, correct pose)
  - DEM/MoGe scale, scale-only fit at R=1000: median 4.8, P10 0.34, P90 31; only 2/24 photos within [0.8, 1.25].
    R=300: median 1.8, P10 0.31, P90 20. R=3000: median 6.0.
  - Strong range compression. Median over photos of the DEM/MoGe ratio by DEM-range bin:
        15-30 m 0.98 | 30-100 m 1.29 | 100-300 m 2.9 | 300-1000 m 6.6 | 1-3 km 9.1 | 3-10 km 11.7 | >10 km 9.8
    So MoGe is roughly metric only within about 100 m, and places distant mountains at a few hundred metres.
  - MoGe over-predicts FOV: median hfov_pred/hfov_true 1.56 (P10 1.14, P90 3.06). Using the model's own
    intrinsics instead of the solved ones barely changes the residual (0.214 vs 0.219 at R1000).
  - moge_b behaves the same (median scale 5.7). da3_b is relative depth (scale ~270): no metric meaning.

  - The 3 photos with no fit even at R3000 are tele shots whose terrain is all > 3 km (wc_0017, 0034, 0048).
    At R1000 six photos have no fit.
  - Design gate "scale residual median < 10% at < 500 m": FAILS. Scale-only, terrain pixels at DEM 15-500 m:
    median 0.217 (22%), and only 26% of photos are individually under 10%. Affine gets close (~0.11) but not under.
  - Warning on the contract: AnchorFit.residualLog is defined as the median over INLIERS. With a +-25% band that
    is always about 0.07 (it measured 0.07 for every config), so it is uninformative. Use the median over all candidates.

    DEFAULT_SPLIT, no split if no fit         8/16                 5.9 %             7/7              23 %

    monotone range-binned mode, near 400      4/16                 2.6 %             7/7              25 %
    power law D = e^a d^b (full range)        5/16                 4.3 %             4/7               9 %
  - Quality gating kills the objects: every quality threshold >= 0.2 removed ALL object detections, because photos
    with big near objects have poor two-sided fits (the objects are one-sided outliers).
  - What I saw looking at the PNGs:
    NAIVE (DEFAULT_SPLIT + IRLS scale): entire distant mountain ranges are magenta on wc_0020, 0034, 0048, 0077
      (30-58 % of the image). These are artefacts from MoGe's range compression and from a scale fitted on the
      foreground. wc_0002 has a large magenta band on a mid-distance slope and along the ridge crest (artefact).
    RECOMMENDED (mode, margin .5, near 150):
      real objects: wc_0046 spruces on the skyline (clean, whole trees), wc_0054 foreground plants (clean),
        wc_0055 cars, van, parking sign, roof overhang, overhead wire, wc_0059 locomotive + catenary pole (the rear
        carriages are cut off by the 150 m radius), wc_0072 overhanging branches, wc_0076 the pylon (very clean).
      missed: wc_0067 lone tree (Far), wc_0088 foreground rock slabs (Far), wc_0072 huts and village, wc_0082
        village and houses (Far; the near forest counts as Terrain, which is arguably right).
      artefacts: wc_0002 thin band along the ridge crest and upper slope (DEM silhouette / eye height), wc_0055 the
        whole snowy car-park surface, wc_0063 a patch of distant snowy slope, wc_0027 part of the rocky tarn shore
        (ambiguous; possibly real rocks the DEM smooths away), wc_0028 the near cliff at bottom left (probably real
        rock not in the DEM).
      clean photos are otherwise empty of Object (good).
    RANGE-BINNED variant: recovers the wc_0067 tree and the wc_0088 rocks, but flags the snow foreground (wc_0067),
      the icy shore (wc_0071), the whole cotton-grass meadow (wc_0063) and the grassy slope (wc_0094). These are
      grazing-angle ground where a ~1 m eye-height or snow-depth error changes the DEM range by tens of percent.
  - A "height above DEM >= 1 m" test (the model point vs the DEM surface at its xy) should kill the grazing-ground
    false positives. Offline I could only build the DEM height field from the view's own visible xyz, and 50-80 % of
    object points had no DEM sample nearby, so the test changed almost nothing. It is NOT evaluated. The app can
    sample the DEM tiles directly, so it is worth trying in P1.
  - No photo in this set contains people, and no hut within 150 m was detected. The design exit criterion
    ("separates people and huts on >= 8/12") could not be tested on this data. Using "real near objects visibly
    separated with little artefact" instead: about 6/7 of the OBJ photos with the recommended variant.

   where the correct view beats the negative)
    moge_l scale-only, residualLogAll:     wrong refs   yaw+-8   yaw+-2   pitch+-2
        R300                                0.72/0.82   0.55/0.58  0.52/0.51  0.56/0.63
        R1000                               0.67/0.79   0.57/0.55  0.54/0.57  0.56/0.57
        R3000                               0.73/0.90   0.63/0.77  0.55/0.63  0.55/0.67
    The inlierFrac signal is similar (R3000: 0.73/0.88 wrong refs, 0.62/0.76 yaw+-8). Affine, power-law, mode and
    one-sided "model beyond DEM" signals are all weaker (0.55-0.70). moge_b is similar; da3_b is weaker (<= 0.67).
    Coverage: R3000 fits exist for 55/55 wrong-ref views and 54/60 yaw+-8 views.
  => Separation is too weak for any absolute accept or veto threshold. It could only serve as a within-photo
     ranking hint for large errors (wrong basin, >= 8 deg). This is consistent with the TM research, where MoGe
     was a verifier only.

PROPOSED VALUES (I did not edit types.ts; the contract is unchanged)
  AnchorFit
    - candidates: model-valid & DEM hit & DEM range in [15 m, 3000 m]; require n >= 2000 grid samples, else no fit.
    - scale: mode of the log(D/d) histogram (Gaussian bandwidth 0.1), refined by the median within +-25 %. This is
      robust to one-sided contamination by near objects. shift = 0. maxRange = 3000.
    - residualLog: report the median |log| over ALL candidates (not inliers; see the warning in 2).
    - quality = inlierFrac * exp(-(residualLogAll / 0.20)^2), using the scale-only IRLS fit at R3000.
      Pass rates at q >= 0.35: correct 41 % (clean photos 61 %, OBJ photos 14 %), wrong refs 16 %, yaw+-8 25 %.
      At q >= 0.15: correct 50 % (OBJ photos 29 %), wrong refs 29 %.
    - ANCHOR_MIN_QUALITY: 0.35 would hide Step Inside on most object-rich photos, which are exactly the photos the
      feature is for. Recommend ANCHOR_MIN_QUALITY = 0.15, used only as a "low trust" label and fade, never as a pose
      check. The pose gate stays "accepted pose only" (design decision 3). The hard gate should be "fit exists"
      (n >= 2000).
  SplitParams (with the mode scale above): objectMargin 0.5, nearRadius 150, minGapM 3.
      (margin 0.6 is equivalent on this set; nearRadius 250 gives 3/16 clean false positives vs 1/16.)
      Split only when a fit exists; otherwise every model-valid pixel is Terrain/Far by DEM range.
      Pixels with model geometry but NO DEM hit (things against the sky) that are within nearRadius are Object. This
      is how the skyline spruces and the plants are caught.
  Optional contract additions to consider (NOT added):
    - SplitParams.minHeightM?: number (~1.0): an Object must stand >= 1 m above the DEM at its own xy (needs a DEM
      sampler in the browser). This is the most promising fix for grazing-ground artefacts. Untested offline.
    - AnchorFit.minRange?: number (15): the DEM range floor used for the fit.
    - A range-dependent calibration (e.g. knots of log scale vs model depth). The monotone binned-mode version
      proved too fragile to recommend.

HONEST LIMITS
  - 30 photos, proxy labels from tags (my grouping), no hand-labelled masks, no people. The split parameters were
    tuned on the same 23 photos they were scored on, so they are optimistic.
  - Resolution: 512-px-wide grid, and the model depth is nearest-sampled from 768 px. Thin structures (wires,
    branches) are under-counted.
  - "Correct" refs carry their own pose error (and some eye-height error), which inflates residuals near the eye.
  - No new inference was run, so the SHARP / TripoSplat candidates are untested here. MoGe-2 is the only metric-ish model.

REVIEWER NOTE (independent re-run, 2026-09-28)
  - Re-ran spike.py, sweep.py, verif2.py, analyze.py (~3 min CPU, no GPU). results_raw/results/sweep/verif2 .json came out
    byte-identical to the builder's files. All headline numbers above reproduce. Dev ids only (asserted). No files found
    written outside tools/nearfield/spike. Coordinate handling (xyz grid pixel centres, z-depth -> ray length with the
    solved intrinsics, OpenCV rays) checked and consistent.
  - CAVEAT the builder did not report: the RECOMMENDED anchor (mode of log(D/d) at R3000) is a FAR-range scale. Because MoGe
    compresses range, it is wrong for near terrain and near objects:
      * design-gate residual on terrain at DEM 15-500 m: mode 0.387 (vs 0.217 for the scale-only IRLS fit);
        per-photo e.g. wc_0076 2.68 (x15), wc_0059 1.37, wc_0054 0.70.
      * mode3000 scale / DEM-to-MoGe ratio at 15-100 m: median 2.0x over 18 photos; off by >2x on 9/18
        (wc_0020 53x, wc_0072 23x, wc_0076 14x, wc_0047 12x, wc_0077 9.7x, wc_0006 6.3x, wc_0059 4.7x).
    So the split "works" partly because near objects get pushed out. Their anchored ENU positions would be many times too
    far. Example: the wc_0076 pylon is classed well but would be placed about 14x too far. The Object/Terrain class maps are
    fine to use as masks. Do NOT use the mode3000 scale to place splats in metres. Placement needs a near-range scale
    (a scale fit on DEM 15-150 m, or a range-dependent curve).
  - Internal inconsistency: the proposed `quality` is computed from the scale-only IRLS fit, but the proposed
    `AnchorFit.scale` is the mode fit. The two scales can differ by several times, so quality does not describe the
    scale that would ship.
