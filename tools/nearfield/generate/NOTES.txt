STEP INSIDE P3: DEM-CONDITIONED GENERATION (research flag only)                               2026-09-28
=========================================================================================================
What it is: the GEN3C / Voyager "3D cache" pattern, done locally.
  1. Render the TRUE scene from a novel camera near the photo eye: the DEM, the photo draped on it from the photo camera
     (only where the photo saw that surface), and the near-field splats.
  2. Every pixel left uncovered is a hole.
  3. LaMa fills ONLY the holes.
  4. Lift the filled pixels into Gaussians with provenance 'generated'. Depth is the DEM range of the novel view where
     the DEM has a surface; otherwise MoGe-2 on the filled view, aligned to the DEM render; otherwise (sky) not lifted.
  5. Merge. The views run sequentially, so each view's cache includes the splats generated before it.

Code
  src/lib/nearfield/generate/
    trajectory.ts       novel cameras (right / left / fwd, clamped to the confidence radius; pivot aim = GEN3C center_facing)
    cache-render.ts     offscreen three.js RGB-D cache. Uses its own override materials and float targets on the engine's renderer;
                        engine.ts is untouched. The terrain group is re-parented for one synchronous call and put back at its index.
    holes.ts            hole mask / stats, monocular alignment, liftGenerated, mergeClouds
    inpaint-client.ts   POST /inpaint (plus /depth with a known fovX)
    pipeline.ts         generateAlongTrajectory
    readout.ts          hover-readout pick; skips generated splats
    remote.ts           GEN3C + LingBot-World v1 request builders (NOT executed; writes .npy / .npz)
    generate.check.ts   npx tsx src/lib/nearfield/generate/generate.check.ts
  src/routes/lab.generate.tsx   /lab/generate?photo=<id>&nearfield=gen (dev only; GT pose only)
  tools/nearfield/service/inpaint.py + /inpaint in app.py (additive), selftest --inpaint-only
  tools/nearfield/generate/shots.mjs   screenshots -> tools/nearfield/shots/gen-*.png + gen-<id>.summary.json

Service issues found and fixed (tools/nearfield/service)
  - Concurrent MPS use from two server threads (an /inpaint overlapping a /depth) aborted the whole process with
    "failed assertion _status < MTLCommandBufferStatusCommitted". Fix: LaMa holds MGR.lock (inpaint.MPS_LOCK).
  - cache.put used a per-pid tmp file, so two identical concurrent requests raced (500 FileNotFoundError). The tmp name
    is now per thread.
  - selftest.py now fires /depth x2 + /inpaint concurrently. Full selftest (--skip-sharp): PASS, 0 failed.

Honest quality judgement (looked at every panel)
  - Small disocclusions on DEM surfaces (the shadows behind the hikers in IMG_7130, ridge-occlusion slivers): LaMa gives
    plausible grass / gravel. The lifted splats sit on the true DEM, and at 10 m the result reads OK in the merged render.
  - Large out-of-frustum bands (the left third of IMG_7130 right-10m, the left edge of IMG_7131): SMEARY. Blurred green and
    blue, ghost fragments of backpacks and people, a floating tree cluster. LaMa on mountains and forests does not make
    believable structure beyond ~50 px from known pixels.
  - The quality of P3 is capped by the P1 scene it starts from. Where the anchor fails (IMG_7086, 7155), whole distant
    ranges are split as Object and placed tens of metres away. They swing with a 10 m step, which opens huge holes,
    and LaMa fills them with generated content. Only run P3 on anchor quality >= ANCHOR_MIN_QUALITY.
  - Monocular fill of no-DEM holes: MoGe alignment is weak (global scale 6-15, residualLog 0.3-0.4 on 7130). Those
    Gaussians are placed at a guessed depth. Consider mono=0 (sky / unknown is simply not lifted).
  - IMG_7131: the hut ~100 m away is not split as Object, so it stays flat on the drape (a P1 matter).
  - The novel-view "photo-eye coverage" sanity is 99.7 % (7131) and 94.4 % (7130). 77 % on 7155 shows near-object
    splats that do not fully cover their masked drape.
