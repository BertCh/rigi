PRE-REGISTRATION DRAFT: pose propagation within a viewpoint (Step Inside P2)
Status: DRAFT, NOT FROZEN, NOT EXECUTED. Written 2026-09-28. Nothing in tools/bench/data_v3 has been
opened, listed, matched or read by this work (not even the manifest). Running it spends held-out data
and needs the user's sign-off. It must also be coordinated with reports/v3-prereg.md, because both use
data_v3 and its blind-verification budget.

1. Hypothesis
  H1 (safety): a propagated pose that passes PROPAGATE_GATE is within 1.0 deg yaw of the target's
     true pose AFTER the existing DEM matcher/cascade refines it from that seed, in >= 95% of gated
     pairs. The false-suggestion rate (gated but wrong) is <= 5%.
  H2 (raw accuracy): the unrefined propagated pose has median geodesic error <= 3 deg and 90th
     percentile <= 5 deg (the seed radius) on gated pairs.
  H3 (recall): among targets that the frozen arm (v034 or t6, whichever is the service default at
     freeze) does NOT accept, propagation from an accepted anchor gives a blind-verified correct pose
     for >= 1 target, with 0 gross errors.
  No accept rule changes under this prereg. Propagated poses stay suggestions (LOW). A later
  accept rule would need its own prereg.

2. Frozen method (stamp at freeze: sha1 of each file)
  - Estimator: tools/nearfield/propagate/run_propagate.py rot_ransac (ALIKED 2048 kp + LightGlue on
    CPU, 1024 px long side, 4 px, 2000 iters, seed 0). The backward estimate (B->A) is computed for
    fwd/bwd.
  - Composition and gate: src/lib/nearfield/propagate.ts proposePose + PROPAGATE_GATE, as in the
    working tree on 2026-09-28: minInliers 40, maxRmsPx 3.5, minOverlap 0.1, maxFwdBwdDeg 1.5,
    maxCycleDeg 1.5, gravity 3/3 deg (only where gravity exists), maxBaselineM 250, methods ["rot"].
    These constants are not re-tuned on data_v3.
  - Anchor: ONLY photos that the frozen arm ACCEPTED under the product rule, with their own vfov.
    Never a GT pose, and never another propagated pose (no chaining).
  - Target intrinsics: EXIF focal only (FF35 diagonal 43.2666 mm), as the app does.
  - Refinement (H1): the frozen matcher/cascade run from the propagated pose as seed, with a
    +/- seedRadiusDeg (5 deg) window. Same eye and DEM (Mapterhorn) as the arm.
  - DA3 is not part of the method (dev: no confidence signal; REPORT.txt).

3. Eligibility (decided from the manifest ONLY, before any pixels are seen)
  - Pairs (A, B) where both are in data_v3, the GPS/position distance is <= 250 m, and A is accepted
    by the frozen arm. Order: every accepted A x every B != A.
  - A pre-check counts eligible pairs from the manifest (lat/lon only) and logs the count before any
    image is opened. If fewer than 10 unordered co-located pairs exist, the run is UNDERPOWERED. Stop
    and report the count only. Do not run a partial test that would spend the set for nothing.
    Expectation from the dev split: Commons co-location is rare (1 pair within 1 km in 50 dev photos).
    data_v3 will most likely be underpowered for this question. In that case the recommended
    held-out set is a NEW one: the user's own camera-roll viewpoints (groups of >= 3 photos within
    250 m) that none of this work has seen. They get the same blind protocol and are frozen by sha1
    before any processing.

4. Ground truth: blind verification (same protocol as reports/test-prereg.md + test-addendum hardening)
  - For each gated B, render overlays at (a) the propagated pose raw, (b) the refined pose, and (c) one
    decoy (the propagated pose with a yaw offset of +/- 3-6 deg, random sign, seeded). Use Mapterhorn
    at the arm's eye, pad the top (PACK_PAD=1), and hide the pose numbers in the header.
  - 2 independent verifier agents per overlay, with a fixed checklist (C1-C4) and their own scratch
    dirs. Retries allowed, and "not-seen" is allowed. Verdict = correct only if both say correct.
    Split verdicts are reported as unsure and counted as NOT correct for H1/H3.
  - Ungated pairs are also rendered at the raw propagated pose (sampled up to 20). This estimates
    what the gate throws away (recall of the gate), not safety.

5. Metrics and decision
  - Primary (H1): verified-correct / gated after refinement, with a Wilson 95% CI. PASS if the point
    estimate is >= 0.95 AND 0 gross errors (> 5 deg or a wrong basin) among gated-and-refined.
  - H2: only where the refined pose is verified correct, the raw-to-refined geodesic difference is
    used as a proxy for raw error. Report the median and p90.
  - H3: count of targets not accepted by the arm whose refined propagated pose is verified correct.
    Report the gross errors separately.
  - Report per-stratum (Swiss / non-Swiss, EXIF GPS / manual) counts, even when they are tiny.
  - Anything computed after unblinding is labelled POST HOC in an addendum.

6. What does NOT change on success
  The frozen product accept rule. A pass allows the product to SHOW the propagated pose as a
  suggestion or seed with a "from neighbouring photo" provenance. Auto-accept needs a separate prereg.

7. Known risks to write down now
  - Anchor error adds directly to the target's error.
  - Parallax from near foreground at 50-250 m baselines. The dev evidence covers only 7-74 m.
  - Moving content (clouds, crowds) could produce many coherent but wrong inliers. This was not
    stress-tested on dev.
  - The dev evidence is n = 6 real ordered pairs from one viewpoint. The thresholds have wide margins
    on negatives (wrong pairs had <= 11 inliers vs gate 40), but little real positive data.
