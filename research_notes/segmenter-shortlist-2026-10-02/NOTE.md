# Segmenter shortlist for Step Inside S1 (2026-10-02)

For the owner. Goal: split classes building, tree/vegetation, person, rock, vehicle, animal. Licences were read today from the repo README/LICENSE pages (URLs below); nothing was downloaded or run. Anything not read is **UNVERIFIED**. Model background is in `research_notes/step_inside_models_2026-09.md` and `object_completion_models_2026-09.md` (SAM 3D, MoGe-2, DINOv3 licence note); not repeated here.

The trap is the dataset, not the code. ADE20K terms (https://ade20k.csail.mit.edu/terms/): images "for non-commercial research and educational purposes", MIT does not own the image copyright, annotations/software CC BSD-3. The terms do not say whether weights trained on it are encumbered, but a product that ships ADE20K-trained weights carries a clear, unresolved risk. COCO annotations are CC BY 4.0 and its images carry per-image Flickr licences (from memory; the terms page could not be fetched, **UNVERIFIED**).

## Recommendation
1. Fastest safe path: **SAM 2** for masks plus **D-FINE or RT-DETR (COCO-only)** for person/vehicle/animal labels, then a small classifier on DINOv3 features (or hand rules from DEM and depth) for building/tree/rock. Everything is Apache-2.0 or DINOv3 License, with no ADE20K weights in the product.
2. Cleanest long-term: **DINOv3 features + our own head**, trained on our own labels. Needs labelling work, not a licence.
3. Before any ADE20K/Cityscapes-trained semantic model is shipped, get a written answer from the dataset owners, or use it only for offline evaluation and never in a product path.
4. Open checks for whoever picks this up: MPS behaviour of every row (none was run), EfficientViT-SAM weights licence, SA-V / SA-1B data terms, COCO image-licence position.
