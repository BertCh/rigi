# Rigi: Market & Business Opportunity (as of Sept 2026)

Scope: addressable markets, business models, channels, partners and segment pull for Rigi (a browser app that auto-aligns the DEM skyline to iPhone mountain photos, overlays peaks, contours and trails, blends map renders, and projects photos onto 3D terrain; Switzerland-focused today). Research done 2026-09-26 with about 22 web searches and fetches. Market-research-firm numbers are low quality and are labelled as such.

## 1. Outdoor/hiking app market size, growth and monetization benchmarks

### Takeaway
The consumer outdoor-navigation category is real but concentrated: a few leaders (Strava, AllTrails, onX, Komoot) take most subscription revenue, at annual prices that have settled around **$60-80/yr for premium tiers**. "Peak identification" is a small niche in comparison, priced at **$5 one-off to $40/yr**. The niche is getting more crowded in 2025-26 with AI "photo → peak name" apps. Consolidation (Bending Spoons buying Komoot, Strava buying FATMAP, Runna and Breakaway) is the main exit route. It also shows that acquirers fold 3D mountain features into their existing subscriptions and do not keep them as standalone products.

### Cited Findings

**Peak-identification niche (Rigi's closest consumer comparables)**
- PeakFinder: **$4.99-5.99 one-off**, AR/panorama drawing, on the App Store since 2010. The Android Alps build shows about 990k downloads and about 9.5k in the last 30 days — [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/app/peakfinder/org.peakfinder.area.alps), [peakspotter comparison](https://peakspotter.app/best-mountain-identifier-app/)
- PeakVisor: free download, then **$39.99/yr or $9.99/mo** for 3D maps, AR peak overlay, trails and ski runs. It has a "PhotoFit" mode for overlaying the skyline on an existing photo — [peakspotter comparison](https://peakspotter.app/best-mountain-identifier-app/), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hiking-and-skiing-peakvisor/id1187259191). There is also a PeakVisor news post titled "[PeakVisor App is Free](https://peakvisor.com/en/news/peakvisor-app-is-free.html)", which I did not read.
- AR AlpineGuide: free with ad-removal IAP ($0.99-14.99), claims **2.5M downloads**, labels photos — [peakspotter](https://peakspotter.app/best-mountain-identifier-app/)
- New AI photo-ID entrants in 2025-26: "Mountain Identifier: Peak Lens" (uses Google Gemini; $1.99-6.99/week or $89.99 lifetime) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mountain-identifier-peak-lens/id6752770531). "Mountain Identifier" by peakspotter (launched Jan 2026, $24.99/yr, AI plus a GPS/compass check) — [peakspotter](https://peakspotter.app/best-mountain-identifier-app/). The comparison page is written by a competitor and may be biased.

### Inferences
- Consumer willingness to pay for "what peak is that" alone is **low**: roughly $5 one-off to $25-40/yr, and it is a feature inside $60-80/yr navigation subscriptions. As a standalone consumer subscription, Rigi would be priced like PeakVisor at best.
- Rigi's differentiator is precise geometric registration of an already-taken photo (sub-degree skyline alignment, projection onto terrain, map blending). The AI apps mostly do "classify the photo → return a name", and PeakVisor PhotoFit requires manual alignment (see §3). This makes accuracy and "wow-quality renders" the pitch, not "identification".
- The FATMAP precedent suggests a buyer (Strava, Komoot/Bending Spoons, AllTrails, onX) would treat Rigi-like technology as an acqui-hire or feature purchase and fold it into its app, not run it standalone.
- Swiss consumer segment: swisstopo's app is free, so charging Swiss users for base maps won't work. The value has to be the photo-fusion layer.

### Gaps
- No reliable, audited market size for "peak ID / photo annotation" as a sub-segment. The analyst reports differ by 4x and are low quality.
- No official AllTrails revenue or subscriber numbers for 2024-26.
- PeakVisor download or revenue figures were not found, and I did not confirm whether PeakVisor recently became fully free (the news title suggests something changed).
- Strava IPO status as of Sept 2026 is unconfirmed.

## 2. Segments and willingness to pay

### Takeaway
B2B/B2G segments where accurate geometry matters and a budget already exists have the strongest pull and the highest willingness to pay: OSINT/law enforcement/verification, SAR, science and monitoring, and tourism operators. Consumer hikers provide volume and virality but pay little. Photographers are a thin, hard-to-reach niche.

### Cited Findings
**OSINT / law enforcement / verification**
- Bellingcat teaches PeakVisor for geolocation (article, toolkit entry, two-part webinar). Investigators must **manually** adjust rotation and FOV, choose candidate viewpoints from contours, and cross-check. Rendered panoramas are "strongly affected by the horizontal field of view setting" and "small viewpoint changes produce large panorama variations" — [Bellingcat 2023-07-13](https://www.bellingcat.com/resources/2023/07/13/more-than-mountaineering-using-peakvisor-for-geolocation/), [Bellingcat toolkit](https://bellingcat.gitbook.io/toolkit/more/all-tools/peakvisor), [webinar](https://www.bellingcat.com/workshops/october-19-20-how-to-use-peakvisor-in-open-source-investigations-webinar/)
- GeoSpy (AI photo geolocation, launched 2023) is sold to police. Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office spent **$85,500**: 2 annual licences with 350 searches at **$5,000 each**, an extra 150,000 searches for $37,500, and a custom model for $38,000. LAPD also evaluated or bought it. Internal guidance treats results as leads, not evidence — [404 Media](https://www.404media.co/cops-are-buying-geospy-ai-that-geolocates-photos-in-seconds/), [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/ai-news/geo-py-ai-photo-geolocation/), [GIGAZINE 2026-02-13](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260213-cops-buying-geospy-ai-geolocates-photos)

**Search & rescue (Rega / SAC)**
- The Rega app sends the caller's coordinates automatically and opens a call to the operations centre. Its map can show or compute coordinates — [Rega app](https://www.rega.ch/en/our-missions/this-is-how-we-help-you/rega-app/regas-emergency-app). Rega and Swiss Alpine Rescue use the "Lifeseeker" phone-location system for searches — [Rega Search & Rescue](https://www.rega.ch/en/our-missions/this-is-how-we-help-you/search-and-rescue). I found **no** evidence that Rega uses photo-based localization today.

**Science (glaciology, snow cover)**
- There is published work on automatic webcam georectification in the Swiss Alps. It solves camera orientation, principal point and FOV from an approximate position plus a high-resolution DEM, without manual GCPs, to map snow cover — [Portenier et al., The Cryosphere 14, 2020](https://tc.copernicus.org/articles/14/1409/2020/). Georeferenced time-lapse glacier monitoring is also active (Tapado, Chile, 2024) — [Remote Sensing Letters 2024](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/2150704X.2024.2337609). The US NPS uses repeat photography for glacier monitoring — [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/articles/glacier-monitoring-techniques.htm)

**Ski touring / guides**
- SLF's White Risk app provides tour planning with slope-angle and avalanche-terrain layers — [PowderGuide](https://powderguide.com/en/magazine/equipment/tour-planning-with-white-risk-experience-report). Skitourenguru is free and rates about 10,000 routes daily from the bulletin plus terrain — [bluewin](https://www.bluewin.ch/en/news/skitourenguru-tool-helps-to-choose-safe-ski-touring-routes-li.3069477). The slope-angle map is therefore covered on 2D maps. Slope-angle overlays drawn on a photo of the face are not offered by these tools (my inference from their feature lists).

**Tourism operators / DMOs (Swiss context)**
- The Swiss hotel sector had a record **43.9M overnight stays in 2025** (+2.6%), of which 22.8M were foreign, with 56.8% occupancy. It was the third record year in a row — [FSO press release](https://www.bfs.admin.ch/asset/en/36398306)
- Rigi Bahnen 2025: **1.02M first-time visitors (+8%)**, net revenue **CHF 39.9M** (+6.5%), EBITDA CHF 13.5M (33.8% margin), 150th anniversary year. 30% of guests are international, and the USA is the top foreign market. Planned investment includes CHF 22M for new Stadler trains by 2030 and a Weggis-Rigi Kaltbad cable car. No digital initiatives were mentioned — [schweizeraktien.net 2026-04-28](https://www.schweizeraktien.net/blog/2026/04/28/rigi-bahnen-viertes-rekordjahr-in-folge-dank-jubilaeum-und-gutem-wetter-72876/)
- Jungfrau Region: 3.91M visitors in 2025, 1.06M to Jungfraujoch, 1.64M to the "Experience Mountains", group profit CHF 78.2M — [swissinfo](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/various/jungfrau-railways-with-record-guest-numbers-and-new-financial-targets/90741519), [bluewin](https://www.bluewin.ch/en/news/jungfrau-railways-with-record-guest-numbers-and-new-financial-targets-3040659.html)

**Real estate (view analysis)**
- My searches did not find a Swiss view-scoring product from Wüest Partner or PriceHubble. Swiss transaction prices were about CHF 23,350/m² in Q1 2026 (+10.6% YoY) per Wüest data as summarised by a search result — [Wüest Partner](https://www.wuestpartner.com/). This is unverified and not directly relevant.

### Inferences (segment ranking by pull and willingness to pay)
1. **OSINT / verification / law enforcement.** The pull is proven: Bellingcat's workflow is manual, and GeoSpy commands $5k/licence/yr. Rigi's automatic, EXIF-independent skyline fit is exactly the missing step. Caveats: data coverage must be global (Switzerland-only is a blocker), and there are ethics and reputation issues with selling to police.
2. **Tourism operators / mountain railways / DMOs.** Visitor volumes are high (Rigi alone has about 1M visitors per year), and operators already pay for webcams and labelled panoramas (§3). Products could be a "label your own photo at the summit" white-label web experience (QR code at the viewpoint), UGC-to-marketing pipelines, and labelled archives. Budget per resort is likely thousands to low tens of thousands of CHF per year (inference; no price data found).
3. **Science / monitoring.** There is technical pull (the TC 2020 paper shows demand for automatic DEM-based georectification), but budgets are small and grant-funded. This is best treated as open-source credibility, citations and partnerships (SLF, WSL, ETH VAW, GLAMOS), not revenue.
4. **SAR.** High value, but the sales cycle is slow and procurement conservative, and the Rega app already sends GPS. Photo-based localization only matters when GPS is missing, for example for a witness who photographs the location of a separate accident. Treat as a partnership or pilot, not a market.
5. **Guides / ski touring.** Slope-angle and avalanche-terrain overlay on a photo of the face is a distinctive feature for trip reports, course teaching and route communication. The segment is small (professional guides), but influential for word of mouth.
6. **Consumer hikers.** Largest volume, lowest willingness to pay ($0-40/yr), crowded with AI ID apps. Best used as the funnel and viral engine, not the revenue core.
7. **Photographers.** Lightroom plugin distribution is weak (Adobe Exchange for LR plugins is described as a "mess" and most authors self-distribute) — [Adobe dev blog](https://blog.developer.adobe.com/en/publish/2022/07/lightroom-classic-plugin-support-for-the-adobe-exchange-for-creative-cloud-14e4a0f690df), [Lightroom Queen forums](https://www.lightroomqueen.com/community/threads/downloading-presets-and-plug-ins-from-adobe-marketplace-how.39338/). Low priority.

### Gaps
- No prices found for DMO or resort digital-viewpoint projects.
- No data on insurance or forensic use of mountain photo geolocation.
- No data on how teachers or schools use such tools.
- No Swiss view-analysis product was found. A deeper search (PriceHubble, IAZI, Swiss "Aussicht" GIS) is needed.
- Rega's interest in photo-based localization is unverified. It would need direct contact.

## 3. Webcam panorama players (Panomax, Roundshot, feratel): auto-labelling, pricing, and the adjacent B2B market

### Takeaway
Panomax markets a "Mountain Labelling" feature (names, heights, distances overlaid on the live 360° image). Panorama webcams are an established B2B market in the Alps built on hardware sale, lease or rent **plus recurring software fees**, with distribution to tourism portals. Public pricing is not disclosed. None of the sources show *automatic*, per-frame DEM registration, which suggests labels are set up once per fixed camera.

### Cited Findings
- Panomax (Austria) "Mountain Labelling" overlays mountain names, heights and distances on live images — [Panomax cable-car page / search summary](https://www.panomax.com/en/webcam-cable-cars). Feature documentation ("Bergbeschriftung") appears in the product brochure — [Panomax info PDF](https://static.messe-essen-digitalmedia.de/uploads/E446/pdf/company/panomax-gmbh-7e357-info-en.pdf)
- Panomax business model: hardware purchase, rental or lease; software fees that stop when the camera is switched off seasonally; paid installation; automatic API feeds to bergfex, wetter.com and others; cost-sharing across several organisations per camera; media partnerships (ServusTV). Pricing is by quote only — [Panomax FAQ](https://www.panomax.com/en/faq)
- Panomax has **800+ active cameras in 24 countries** (about March 2025, via a partner announcement) — [Digitur Media 2025-03-05](https://digitur.no/en/2025/03/05/we-are-now-a-panomax-partner/). This is a secondary source.
- Roundshot (Seitz, Switzerland) sells Livecam hardware of up to 66 MP and a Livecam app network. Its customers include Zürich Tourism and Neuchâtel. A price-list page exists but I could not read its contents — [Roundshot webcams](https://www.roundshot.com/en/webcams.html/12), [price lists](https://www.roundshot.com/xml_1/internet/en/application/d137/f160.cfm)
- feratel runs a large European webcam and weather network for destinations — [feratel webcams](https://www.feratel.com/en/webcams)

### Inferences
- The adjacent B2B opportunity is not to compete on cameras but to sell **registration as a service**: automatically register any webcam or photo archive to the DEM, and keep labels, contours and trails accurate when cameras drift or pan. Two further uses: reuse the imagery for snow-line and cloud analysis for operators and science (TC 2020), and let visitors' phone photos at the same viewpoint inherit the labels.
- Panomax and Roundshot are potential **partners or acquirers**, not just competitors. They own the resort relationships.

### Gaps
- No public prices for Panomax, Roundshot or feratel. Industry anecdotes suggest thousands of euros in hardware plus annual fees, but I found no citable source.
- Whether Panomax labelling is automatic (DEM-registered) or manually set up per camera is unconfirmed.
- Roundshot or feratel label features were not checked.

## 4. Social/sharing trends

### Takeaway
Every peak-ID app advertises "share to social", and AI photo-ID apps (2025-26) use aggressive weekly subscriptions. Some consumers want annotated summit photos, but I found **no hard data** on sharing volumes or viral examples.

### Cited Findings
- PeakLens and Peak Lens market sharing of annotated skyline photos to social networks — [PeakLens](https://www.peaklens.com/), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mountain-identifier-peak-lens/id6752770531)
- Strava's 2024 API terms forbid showing a user's Strava data to other users via third-party apps, which limits "annotated photo in a public feed" integrations — [Strava press](https://press.strava.com/articles/updates-to-stravas-api-agreement)

### Inferences
- Shareable output (a labelled photo with a subtle "Rigi" watermark and a link back to an interactive 3D view) is the natural viral loop for a web app. The share has to be an image or link the user posts themselves, not a Strava-feed integration.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data on Instagram or Strava posts with peak annotations, and no documented viral case.

## 5. Distribution channels (App Store vs web/PWA, plugins, API integrations, Swiss partners)

### Takeaway
Web or PWA with share links fits the B2B, white-label and OSINT segments, and it avoids a crowded App Store niche. Strava's API is closed to this kind of use. Komoot is now run for monetization by Bending Spoons. swisstopo's open data (free commercial use since March 2021) makes a Switzerland-first product legally cheap. The best Swiss partner channels are mountain railways, DMOs and Switzerland Tourism.

### Cited Findings
- Since 1 March 2021, swisstopo geodata (swissALTI3D, swissSURFACE3D, national maps) is Open Government Data. Commercial use is allowed free of charge, with attribution "© swisstopo" — [swisstopo OGD](https://www.swisstopo.admin.ch/en/free-geodata-ogd), [terms](https://www.swisstopo.admin.ch/en/terms-of-use-free-geodata-and-geoservices)
- Strava's API bars displaying data to other users and training AI on it (Nov 2024) — [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2024/11/223-strava-revises-api-rules-to-restrict-data-sharing-and-training-ai-models-using-user-data/)
- Komoot moved route sync behind a paywall right after the acquisition, and critics described the pricing as hostile to new customers — [the5krunner 2025-03-11](https://the5krunner.com/2025/03/11/komoot-confirms-we-dont-want-any-new-customers-in-ridiculous-pricing-move/)
- Lightroom Classic plugins can be distributed through Adobe Exchange (ZXP support since CC desktop 5.8, July 2022), but uptake is poor — [Adobe dev blog](https://blog.developer.adobe.com/en/publish/2022/07/lightroom-classic-plugin-support-for-the-adobe-exchange-for-creative-cloud-14e4a0f690df). Existing geo plugins are indie (Jeffrey Friedl's) — [regex.info](https://regex.info/blog/lightroom-goodies/gps)
- swisstopo's own app has 1.2M downloads and about 75k uses per day, and is free — [swisstopo](https://www.swisstopo.admin.ch/en/an-app-as-smart-as-swisstopo)

### Inferences
- Priority channels: (1) a web app with shareable links and embeds; (2) white-label or embed deals with mountain railways and DMOs (QR codes at viewpoints, "label your summit photo"); (3) OSINT community channels (Bellingcat toolkit listing, workshops); (4) the App Store later, only if on-device AR or camera integration is needed.
- swisstopo is more a data source and possible showcase or innovation partner than a distribution channel. Switzerland Tourism is a promotional partner.

### Gaps
- No info on Komoot's public API or partner programme after the acquisition.
- No info on Switzerland Tourism or swisstopo startup and innovation programmes, for example swisstopo "Geo innovation" calls.
