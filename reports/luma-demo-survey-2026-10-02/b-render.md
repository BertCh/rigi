# Survey B: luma.gl rendering and visual demos → lessons for Rigi (2026-10-02)

Scope: luma master (export dated 2026-10-02, a `git archive` of luma `origin/master` 931ec1c53 (2026-10-02)) showcase, experimental and API rendering demos, plus `modules/{effects,scene,text,splats}`, compared with Rigi on vendored luma `10.0.0-alpha.2-rigi.6`. Read-only. Nothing was run in a browser or on a GPU; the claims come from reading code. Four parallel readers wrote parts 1–4 (appended below). The coordinator spot-checked the load-bearing claims: `engine.ts:1017`, `splats-luma.ts:65,70,76,260`, master `gpu-fft-utils.ts` and `flags/index.ts:108`.

**Availability headline.**
- **Nothing below needs a vendor bump.** In `@luma.gl/effects` the file set matches master, and the WGSL of every recommended pass is identical. `@luma.gl/experimental` ships gpu-raster, oit, shadows, lucim and simulation in its dist. `@luma.gl/splats` in rigi.6 has everything master has, plus #3340. `PresentationContext`, `VideoTexture` and the canvas `toneMapping` option are in rigi.6 core and engine.
- **Not vendored:** `@luma.gl/scene`, `@luma.gl/text` and `@luma.gl/gltf`. Scene and text are private upstream. No lesson below needs them.
- **Caveat:** importing directly from `@luma.gl/experimental` (gpu-raster contours, overviews) adds a direct dependency on a private, unpublished package. Rigi already imports gpu-raster ops through `gpu/core/luma.ts`, so the precedent exists.

### Already adopted (one line each)
- Tone mapping: PBR Neutral after an Oklab band transfer (`composite.ts:451-484`).
- 8× sampler anisotropy on photo, imagery, tiles3d and terrain.
- The interactive 1× MSAA drop.
- gpu-raster morphology, edges, Otsu and threshold; `GPUFiniteDifference2D`; `GPUConvolution`.
- Compile once and encode into the frame encoder (`flow.ts`); packed summary readbacks (geo-query).
- `GPUSort` splat sort; luma paged splat renderer + RAD (#3340), which **reversed the LF6 negative result**; the 2D AA kernel matches `splats.ts`; SH degree 0.
- Copy path for live video with rVFC; Cache API → IndexedDB store; `pointGlow`; heightFog → Nebelmeer.

### Rejected (one line each)
- TAA: camera moves every frame, forward-Z, determinism.
- GTAO/SSAO: the relief sky-view factor is the better estimator for terrain.
- Shadow maps: horizon sweeps already do this; watch item for tiles3d only.
- OIT/A-buffer: splats are sorted and the multi-drape blends inside one shader.
- ACES and bloom: they fight the photo match and the map look.
- SSR, `selectionOutline`: already rejected in LF.
- Native `texture_external`: it would need a second binding variant everywhere.
- `@luma.gl/text` SDF labels: private, no placement or collision.
- `GPUJacobiPCG`, Poisson blending, binned histogram/percentile for haze.
- FFT ocean lakes: WebGPU-only, lakes are distant.
- Globe, scene/ANARI, glTF PBR/IBL, spectral caustics, LuCIM 3D, clustered deferred lighting.
- `GPUVirtualGeometrySelection`: already rejected.

## Per-demo notes

**raster-lab** (7.7k lines; `@luma.gl/experimental/gpu-raster` + `GPUCommandGraph` + `DrawCommandBuffer`)
- Shows: EO-style raster analytics on storage buffers. Band math/NDVI, validity-aware stats, histogram plus a global multi-tile histogram/percentile merge, Otsu, morphology, dense, cross-tile and connected components with per-region measurements, GPU contours drawn indirectly, tile halo fill for neighbourhood ops across tiles, and float and categorical overviews.
- Structure: a `GPURasterTileSource` adapter does decode only and allocates no GPU memory (`raster-tile-source.ts:21`). One explicitly submitted graph per settings change.
- Rigi: already on the same primitives for haze, relief and skyline. Unused here: contours, tile halo/cache, overviews, region measurements.

**poisson-lab** (`GPUJacobiPCG`, CSR 5-point Laplacian)
- Manufactured solution with an analytic error check (`poisson-problem.ts:15-19`). The solve is encoded on `device.commandEncoder`, and the solution buffer is the renderer's source with no readback (`poisson-lab-engine.ts:85-88`).
- Rigi: no PDE consumer of this size (see Rejected). Worth keeping in mind only if Step Inside ever does depth-membrane hole filling at full resolution.

**spectral-wave-lab** (`GPUFFT2D`, `Computation`)
- Exact-in-time spectral evolution of the 2D wave equation, rendered in physical and Fourier space from the same buffers.
- Pedagogical only. Its lesson (graph compiled once, encoded per frame) is already in Rigi.
- Depends on the FFT that master still has broken on Metal (lesson 1).

**tempest-ocean** (`SpectralOceanSimulation` from `@luma.gl/experimental`, `createBloomCompositeShaderPass`, `toneMapping`, `ShaderPassRenderer`)
- Rendering: Tessendorf/Phillips FFT ocean with choppy displacement and foam history, Schlick fresnel sky reflection, sun specular, and foam contours anti-aliased with `fwidth` (`app.ts:791-811`).
- Output and capture: HDR rgba16float scene → bloom → tone map with display-dependent max luminance; HDR screenshot capture (`tempest-ocean-capture.ts`).
- Rigi: tone mapping yes; FFT water no (lesson 4); HDR canvas no (lesson 5).

**vector-field-lab** (`GPUCommandGraph`, `Computation`, `GPUFiniteDifference3D`, `@luma.gl/scene/raymarch`)
- 40³ analytic fields; gradient, Laplacian, divergence and curl on the GPU; four raymarched linked views and volumetric arrow glyphs.
- No Rigi use: the field is 2D and CPU-shared with WebGL, and `scene` is not vendored.

**globe** (`makeAnimationLoop`, both adapters, GLSL + WGSL twin shaders)
- Textured sphere with water-mask specular, fresnel rim, cubemap skybox and directional + point lights. Shows dual-language shaders kept side by side, which is Rigi's rule 4 practice already.
- No lesson.

# Part 2: post-effects demos and modules/effects (fork 2)

Scope: showcase/dof, showcase/postprocessing, experimental/{bloom, antialiasing, advanced-effects, deferred-rendering, a-buffer, shadow-map}, modules/effects.
`$L` = <luma master 931ec1c53>, `R` = mt-image.

**Availability.** Every `@luma.gl/effects` pass file in master is in vendored rigi.6 (same file set; WGSL shader lines identical for TAA, dof, gtao, hdr-auto-exposure, tone-mapping, fxaa, bloom, tiltshift and depth-aware-blur). The experimental renderers the demos use (`ABufferRenderer`, `WBOITRenderer`, `ShadowMapRenderer`, `GBuffer`, `ClusteredLightGrid`, `GPUConvolutionBloom`, `ComparisonSplitter`, `createContactShadowCompositeShaderPass`) are all in the vendored `@luma.gl/experimental` `index.d.ts`. **None of this needs a vendor bump.** `@luma.gl/experimental` is installed only as a transitive dependency of splats (vendor/luma/README.md), so importing from it is a new direct dependency on a private, unpublished package.

**Shader-language split** (it matters for rule 4: both engines). The `postprocessing/*` filters (fxaa, toneMapping, bloom, tiltShift, gaussianBlur, vignette, …) and screen-space `dof`, `height-fog` and `selection-outline` ship WGSL and GLSL. TAA, GTAO, SSAO, hdr-auto-exposure, motion-blur, outlines, SSGI, SSR, volumetric/clustered fog and depth-aware-blur are **WGSL only**, as are the shadow-map, advanced-effects and deferred-rendering demos (labelled "WebGPU-only": `$L/examples/experimental/shadow-map/app.ts:190`, `advanced-effects/app.ts:316`, `deferred-rendering/app.ts:319`).

**Depth convention trap.** The screen-space passes assume forward, finite-far depth: the TAA sky test is `currentDepth >= 0.99999` (`camera-reprojection-temporal-antialiasing.ts:98`), and DoF reconstructs linear depth from `depthRange` near/far (`dof.ts:74-77`). Rigi uses reversed infinite-far Z, sky = 0 (`R/src/lib/deck-webgpu/README.md` §Depth). Its colour depth is also a **transient 4× MSAA depth32float** (`targets.ts:15-16`, discarded after the resolve). So no depth-reading luma pass drops in as-is: each one needs a sky-test and linearisation port, plus a resolved or `texture_depth_multisampled_2d` depth.

## Per-demo notes

- **api/render-bundles** (512 lines, WebGPU only): N cubes, each with its own uniform buffer; bundle rebuilt only on draw-count change; toggle compares CPU. Rigi's `render-bundle.ts` is more complete (attachment signature keys, MSAA 4x/1x variants, identity-keyed invalidation, Dawn-tested). Lesson 2.
- **api/multi-canvas** (642): one Device, React-managed canvases, each a `PresentationContext`; also accepts an external `presentationDevice`. Works on WebGL too (luma copies from an offscreen default canvas). Lesson 1.
- **api/video-texture** (768): `VideoTexture` (engine), mediabunny decode to `VideoFrame`, rVFC pump, native external texture on WebGPU, copied texture on WebGL; the graph re-compiles only on size change. Lesson 5.
- **api/texture-compressed** (335) / **texture-tester** (1747): KTX2 via ktx-parse, ASTC 4x4 or BC5 by feature; ETC2 and float KTX2 noted as failing. Tester is a format matrix, useful as a reference if lesson 4 is attempted (block alignment rules).
- **api/texture-3d** (300): PicoGL port, 16³ r8unorm Perlin volume, ray-slab render. Lesson 3 (noise volume).
- **api/texture-sampling** (1318): sampler playground. Lesson 11 (done).
- **experimental/volumetric-fire-forge** (2852): `VolumetricFireSimulation` (experimental, 3D velocity/combustion/obstacle textures), depth-aware HDR raymarch compositor with self-shadow transmittance, bloom composite + `toneMapping` from `@luma.gl/effects`. Lesson 3 takes the compositor, not the solver.
- **experimental/virtual-geometry-canyon** (2492): `GPUVirtualGeometrySelection` + indirect draws over a 42 M-triangle procedural canyon (already evaluated and rejected for terrain LOD, `deck-webgpu/README.md:335-340`). New angles: per-vertex geomorph (lesson 6); canyon-wind is WebAudio whistles, not a wind field (Rigi's LF4 wind is already richer); canyon-camera is a spline fly-through with clamped yaw/pitch offsets (`canyon-camera.ts:81-88`), nothing beyond Rigi's fly mode.
- **experimental/lucim-volume-lab** (1067): GPU threshold → 3D morphology → connected components → region stats on a CT phantom, one reusable graph, tri-planar view. Lesson 8.
- **experimental/spectral-caustics** (986): lesson 10, skip.
- **experimental/text-space-crawl** (1172): extruded vs bitmap vs SDF vs MSDF text in perspective, via `@luma.gl/text` (+ `@luma.gl/arrow`). Lesson 9, rejected.
- **modules/text**: `TextRenderer`, `GPUTextResources`, `buildSdfFontAtlas` / `buildMsdfFontAtlas` / `loadMsdfFontAtlas`, `measureFontAtlasText`, text-3d extrusion. Private, no label placement.
