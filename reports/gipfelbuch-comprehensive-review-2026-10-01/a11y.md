# Gipfelbuch review: accessibility, theming, print, responsive, fonts, routing

Static analysis only (no browser, no render lock). Tree at 6c02f47 plus uncommitted work. Every item below was checked in source; contrast ratios were computed with the WCAG formula against the actual `--gb-paper` mix in theme.css:15 (W 96% + YY 4%).

## Bugs

1. **The print rule shows hidden native sliders.** `swiss/print.css:42-45` forces `opacity:1 !important` on every `[class*="opacity-0"]`. That also matches the transparent `<input type=range>` overlays that sit on top of hand-drawn sliders: `viz/labels.tsx:156` (the shared hand range) and `pages/camera-prior.tsx:964`. In print, a native slider draws over the sketch.
   Fix: limit the rule to reveal wrappers (for example `[data-reveal]`), or add `print:hidden` to those two inputs. Reveal, Figure, Steps and Trio already carry `print:opacity-100`, so the rule is redundant for them anyway.
2. **The print edition leaves every `<Details>` closed.** The block is defined at `viz/explain.tsx:78` and used 19 times in `src/lib/gipfelbuch/pages`. Neither print.css nor a `beforeprint` handler opens them, so the "Details for engineers" content is missing from `/gipfelbuch/print`.
   Fix: add a `beforeprint` listener that sets `open` on `.gb-swiss details`, or add `@media print { details::details-content { content-visibility: visible } }` with a JS fallback.
3. **SVG buttons are nested inside `role="img"`.** Children of an img role are presentational, so these buttons are hidden from screen readers but still take focus:
   - `pages/photo-workspace.tsx:956` (svg at :894)
   - `pages/camera-prior.tsx:360` (svg at :262)
   - `pages/camera-roll.tsx:1371` (svg at :1335)
   - `pages/camera-roll.tsx:1540` (svg at :1438)

   The same groups (and `pose-estimate.tsx:1128`) handle only `Enter`, not Space, and have no `aria-pressed`. `pages/accept-rule.tsx:566` has `role="button"` but no action at all (hover/focus only).
   Fix: drop `role="img"` on interactive SVGs and use `role="group"` plus `aria-label`. Handle Enter and Space, and add `aria-pressed`. On accept-rule, use `role="img"` or a focusable `<title>`.
4. **Undefined tokens.**
   - `var(--gb-blue)` at `pages/terrain-snapping.tsx:920` and `var(--gb-faint)` at `pages/dem-source.tsx:245,253` are not defined anywhere. The likely intent is `--gb-water` and `--nb-faint`. The labels fall back to the inherited fill.
5. **`--accent` is missing in the print route.** `ConceptPage.tsx:192` sets `--accent` per group. `gipfelbuch.print.tsx:37-95` (SheetBody) does not.
   - About 60 page usages of `var(--accent)` lose their colour in the printed edition.
   - `pages/viewport-inference.tsx:1587`: the selected-photo outline disappears (`outline: 2px solid var(--accent)` becomes invalid).
   - Fix: set `--accent` on SheetBody's `<section>`.
6. **`--nb-faint` text fails AA.** It measures 3.63:1 on paper and 3.44:1 on paper-deep. Used for text at:
   - `notebook/NotebookMap.tsx:277,333,394`
   - `pages/camera-roll.tsx:1188,1430,1670,1733`
   - `pages/dem-anchoring.tsx:509,530,1159,1202,1877`

   Fix: use `--gb-secondary` (6.98:1) for text and keep `--nb-faint` for strokes.
7. **SiteNav on paper is under contrast.** The Gipfelbuch routes render `<SiteNav>` without `variant="paper"`:
   - Call sites: `gipfelbuch.index.tsx:109`, `ConceptPage.tsx:194`, `gipfelbuch.$concept.tsx:30`.
   - Inactive items use `text-white/55`, which `.gb-swiss` re-inks to 55% ink: 3.99:1 at 12px (`site/SiteNav.tsx:34`).
   - The `paper` variant (`SiteNav.tsx:15-41`) has no callers.
   - Fix: pass `variant="paper"`, or raise the alpha to 0.65 or more (5.55:1).
8. **Pickers that do not work from the keyboard or with assistive tech.**
   - `pages/baseline-pipeline.tsx:1275`: a clickable `<tr onClick>` with no tabIndex or key handler.
   - `pages/viewport-inference.tsx:1577`: a photo picker button with no `aria-pressed`; selection is shown only by an accent outline.

## Risks

9. **Global CSS leaks out of the Gipfelbuch.** `swiss/theme.css` is loaded through `palette.ts:9` and stays in the document after SPA navigation. Its `@import "./print.css"` brings unscoped rules:
   - `@page { size: A4; margin … }` (print.css:10-21)
   - `nav, [data-site-nav] { display:none !important }` (print.css:78-81)
   - root `::view-transition-*` durations (theme.css:315-325)

   After a visit to the Gipfelbuch, printing any other route drops every `<nav>` and uses Tschichold margins. On the index itself, the hand table of contents is a `<nav>` (`gipfelbuch.index.tsx:52`), so it vanishes in print.
   Fix: scope the rules to `.gb-swiss nav` / `.gb-swiss [data-site-nav]`, and move `@page` behind a named page (`.gb-swiss { page: gb }`, `@page gb {…}`).
10. **Dark theme: no `color-scheme: light` on the paper.** `.gb-swiss` (theme.css:12) and `.nb-book` (notebook.css:38) set paper tokens but inherit `color-scheme: dark` from `[data-theme=dark]`. Native checkboxes (`pose-estimate.tsx:729,866`, `baseline-pipeline.tsx:2144`) and other UA widgets render dark on paper.
    - Other inherited dark bits: the dark `theme-color` meta (`__root.tsx`), and `body` stays `--rigi-ink` (visible on overscroll).
    - The ThemeToggle in the nav has no visible effect on these pages.
    - `--rigi-*` and `--color-white/black` are correctly re-inked inside `.gb-swiss`, and the dark island in `rigi.tsx:682` re-declares the aliases (`tafel.css:7-8`). No dark-token leak into the paper was found apart from the above.
    - Fix: add `color-scheme: light` to both roots.
11. **Status blazes are colour-only.** "flagged" (mountain) and "research" (alpine) are the same shape with a red or navy middle bar (`swiss/Waymark.tsx:97`). On the index the status name is `sr-only` (`gipfelbuch.index.tsx:77-81`), and the index legend (`LEGEND_ITEMS`, :39) has no status key.
    Fix: add a status key, or a visible hand label or tooltip.
12. **Fetch failures leave a permanent skeleton.**
    - `viz/real.tsx:194-210` (`useJson`) only calls `console.warn`; RealPhoto and DemPatch pulse forever.
    - `pages/peak.tsx:897` and `pages/tap-a-peak.tsx:819` cache a rejected promise forever and never check `r.ok`.
    - `camera-roll.tsx:1273`, `eye-rule.tsx:538` and `photo.tsx:111` skip `r.ok`.
    - Fix: one shared `useJson` with an error state, plus a hand-lettered "couldn't load" note.
13. **Small text in hand fonts.**
    - Body text is Playpen Sans everywhere (`theme.css:60`); `.gb-print` (the escape back to typeset text) is used only 4 times.
    - 11px is common: `TYPE.micro` (125 uses), `text-[11px]` (62), `margin-note.css:13`.
    - 10px HTML text: `notebook/notes.tsx:125`, `pages/photo.tsx:514`, `swiss/SheetFrame.tsx:47`.
    - Fixed-size SVG text: 10px at `Legend.tsx:116`, `ScaleBar.tsx:112,125`; 10.5px at `Marks.tsx:369,379`.
    - Viewbox-scaled SVG labels go below 10px on phones, for example `real.tsx:815` (`10.5*k`) and `:1296`.
    - The Details body is 13px Playpen (`explain.tsx:100`).
    - Fix: set a 12px floor for hand faces, use `.gb-print` for captions and Details bodies, and consider a reader toggle for typeset text.
14. **Fonts.** All faces are self-hosted under `public/fonts/gipfelbuch` (1.4 MB on disk), every face uses `font-display: swap`, and latin and latin-ext are split with unicode-range.
    - Per page, the latin hand faces are about 460 KB: Playpen 197 KB, Shantell 174 KB, Caveat 75 KB, Patrick Hand SC 14 KB. Source Serif (122 KB) is added when there is math.
    - None of them is preloaded. `__root.tsx:49` preloads Fira Sans 400, which the Gipfelbuch body does not use.
    - `GB Hand Body` has no metric-matched fallback (`fonts.css:388-390`), so the swap causes layout shift.
    - Unused: `GB Sans Condensed` (4 files, about 101 KB; `--gb-font-condensed` is never referenced).
15. **Interactive figures print a single state.** `Stages` (`explain.tsx:315-460`) prints whichever stage is current; `Compare` prints mid-wipe.
    Fix: under `print`, render all stages stacked, or the last stage plus captions.
16. **SVG presentation attributes that use `var()`.**
    - About 35 direct `fill=`/`stroke="var(--…)"` attributes: SheetMap.tsx ×13, Blattuebersicht ×3, Waymark ×2, Signpost ×2, ScaleBar ×2, HachureRule ×2, ContourField ×2, Legend, Marks, real.tsx, explain.tsx:204, step-inside ×2, AutoVisual.
    - One `fill="color-mix(…)"` (`Signpost.tsx:110`).
    - Many more pass `color="var(…)"` into Ink components.
    - Current Chrome, Firefox and Safari parse presentation attributes as CSS, so these resolve.
    - Nothing in the Gipfelbuch serializes SVG (no XMLSerializer, toDataURL or outerHTML), so risk is low.
    - The real failure mode is an undefined token: the attribute becomes unset, giving an inherited fill or no stroke (see items 4 and 5).
    - In forced-colors mode, `theme.css:281-287` remaps every fill and stroke to currentColor, which wipes out colour encodings (accepted trade-off).
17. **`contrast.check.ts` coverage.**
    - Its paper model is stale (:49-50 uses W90/YY10 and LG20; theme.css uses 96/4 and 9). That is conservative for dark inks but should be read from the CSS.
    - It covers 8 text inks on paper and paper-deep, scans the two CSS files for relief or MG used as text, and checks inks.ts ↔ Brezine parity.
    - It does not cover:
      - colours set in TSX (nb-faint 3.63:1, SiteNav ink/55 3.99:1, `opacity-60` text);
      - `--nb-alpine` (4.48:1, just under);
      - text over photos (`PHOTO_LABEL` halo, `explain.tsx:315`);
      - the dark plate (`rigi.tsx:682`);
      - SVG `<text>` fills;
      - non-text contrast (focus rings, the slider track at 22% ink);
      - forced-colors.
18. **Production gating and errors.**
    - `dev.gipfelbuch-sheet.tsx:20` is a runtime `import.meta.env.DEV` gate. The route still ships in production and renders a bare "dev only" page.
    - Out of scope, but `dev.meta.tsx` has no gate at all.
    - `PageBoundary` (`loadPage.tsx:47`) shows the raw `err.message` to readers and has no retry for a chunk-load failure.

## Cleanup

19. **Heading hierarchy.**
    - The print route has two h1s (`Cartouche.tsx:36`, `Colophon.tsx:166`).
    - Sheet titles are h2 (`print.tsx:63`) and so are Beat headings (`explain.tsx:56`), so the print outline is flat.
    - Index chapter titles are h2 inside the "Blattübersicht" h2 (`Blattuebersicht.tsx:346`).
    - `OntologyPanel.tsx:25` jumps from h2 to h4.
20. **Reduced motion.** It is handled well:
    - `revealsImmediately` and `useTime` in `hooks.ts`;
    - Stages and Compare auto-play;
    - `sheetTransition`;
    - `nb-draw` and `nb-hm` (notebook.css:117-178).

    Leftovers:
    - 29 `animate-pulse` skeletons have no `motion-reduce:animate-none`, for example `step-inside.tsx:783,983,1519`, `terrain-sampler.tsx:855,1010,1128,1197`, `camera-prior.tsx:426,494,1311`.
    - The stage fade at `explain.tsx:430`.
21. **Alt text and names.** `DemoImage` (`viz/DemoImage.tsx`) has no callers and defaults to `alt=""`. Photos are otherwise `alt=""` inside labelled buttons, which is correct.
    - The RealPhoto label is generic: `aria-label="${id} with measured overlays"` (`real.tsx:540`).
    - Pickers are labelled only "demo-03": `real.tsx:1065`, `viewport-inference.tsx:1582`, `camera-roll.tsx:1375,1544`.
    - SVG coverage is good: of 181 `<svg>`, 178 carry `role`/`aria-label`/`aria-hidden`; the other 3 are a `<title>`-labelled index inside a labelled nav, a comment, and a conditional wrapper.
22. **Responsive.** There is no hard overflow.
    - `min-w-[…]` values are at most 250px inside flex-wrap.
    - Container queries are used (Stages, Ledger, accept-rule).
    - `.tafel-root` is 100vw (`tafel.css:19`) and is clipped by `overflow-x-clip` on ConceptPage plus `overflow-x:hidden` on body.
    - At phone width, `SheetFrame px-4` (:76) plus page `px-6` give 40px per side (the outer 16px rule is met, but content is 295px wide at 375px).
23. **Routing and SEO.**
    - An unknown `$concept` renders an in-component "Off the map" page (`$concept.tsx:27-46`) with the title "Concept · Rigi Gipfelbuch". There is no `notFound()` and no real 404 (`ssr:false`).
    - Per-page head has a title only: no description or og tags.
    - The print title has no "Rigi" (`print.tsx:28`).
    - `total="19"` is hard-coded (`index.tsx:113`).
    - All 19 node ids have a page module, and the print route iterates all of them.
    - SSR safety is moot: every Gipfelbuch route is `ssr:false`. localStorage access is wrapped in try/catch (`useNotebookPhoto.ts:25-50`).

## Positives verified
- Global `.gb-swiss :focus-visible` red outline (theme.css:148), plus the range focus ring.
- Native `<details>`/`<summary>`.
- Stages has Pause and `aria-pressed` tabs.
- The Compare slider has `role=slider` and arrow keys.
- forced-colors and prefers-contrast branches exist.
- The breadcrumb Waymark shows its status text visibly.
