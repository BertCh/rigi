## Kept on purpose (verified 2026-10-02)

Several of these are runtime guards or the reference a GPU path is checked against. **Do not delete them as dead code.**

**Fallbacks and switches**
- `?webgpu=off`, `?gpu=off` (compute kill switch); the WebGL2 deck engine as the non-WebGPU fallback.
- Splat sort: GPU sort is the WebGPU default; `gpu/splat-sort/fallback.ts` and the worker sort (`nearfield/splat-sort.worker.ts`) serve WebGL2 and failures. `?splatRenderer=rigi` keeps the Rigi splat shader next to luma's stack.
- CPU backends of `src/lib/nn` (`nn/cpu.ts`): the reference every GPU kernel is checked against, and the non-WebGPU path for sky and people masks.
