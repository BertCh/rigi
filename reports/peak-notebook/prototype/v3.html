Blatt 10 / 19EvidenceSkyline

Where the mountain meets the sky.

The skyline is the one line a phone photo reliably shows. Rigi traces it column by column, and says how sure it is of each, so the pose search can trust the crests and ignore the glare.

01 · The idea

Every column casts one vote, with a confidence.

The pose search never sees the photo. It gets one number per column: the height of the skyline, plus how much to trust it.

A good detector is honest per column: sure on a clean crest, silent behind a roof.

Fig. 1Each tick is one of 800 columns; its height is the weight it gets in the solve. Gaps are columns that abstain.
02 · Where it fails

Unsure columns abstain. Occluders are the real enemy.

Glare and dark foregrounds only remove votes. A person on the ridge is worse: it looks like a real edge.
