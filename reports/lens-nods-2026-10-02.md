## Facts behind the copy

- **Arisia** is luma.gl's own name for its gpgpu stack (`GPUProgram` lowering to `GPUCommandGraph` on WebGPU), used in luma PR bodies (#3237, #3250, about 21 `codex/arisia-*` PRs, 2026-09-14 to 09-17), branch names and `dev-docs/roadmaps/arisia-*.md`. Code identifiers were never renamed. No Arisia-labelled work after 2026-09-17.
- **Ploor** (a future planner/inspector over lowering decisions) is mentioned twice in luma's gpu-core docs; its branch `codex/arisia-ploor-inspector` is empty. No other Lensman names exist in any visgl repo.
- **deck.gl has no compute graph.** Rigi's own `ComputeGraph` (`src/lib/gpu/core/graph.ts`) runs luma's `GPUCommandGraph` on the render device and deck-webgpu draws from its buffers. Copy must never say deck runs a compute graph.
- `GraphInspection` (`src/lib/gpu/core/inspect.ts`) carries node kind, condition, workload bound and p50 CPU/GPU timings, but not luma's lowering decisions.
- Naming limits (not legal advice): no quotes, characters or art from the novels; no "Lensman" in the UI (a LENSMAN trademark exists in photographic goods). Generic words (lens, clear ether) are fine.
